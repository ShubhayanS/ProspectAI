## C. Hard Blockers

1. **Relocate anywhere in US, project-to-project.** Posting is explicit: "willing to relocate anywhere in the US on a client project-to-project basis, as this is an onsite, in-office position." This means Philadelphia is not guaranteed — Selin could be placed in Cleveland, Houston, or anywhere else for each project rotation. Incompatible with Philly/remote preference.
2. **Consulting body-shop model.** "Tech Consulting" is a generic-named staffing firm deploying workers to client sites. No visibility into what actual work, industry, or team you land on. Career development is client-dependent.
3. **$35–38/hr is low for MS-level.** ~$72–79K annualized. Below typical MS Data Science market rate for Philadelphia or comparable US markets.

## D. Recommendation

**Skip.** Skills match, but the engagement model (nomadic relocation, unknown client assignments, below-market pay) conflicts with stated preferences and career goals. If Selin is open to consulting and relocation, she should target named firms (Accenture, Deloitte, etc.) with better pay, career paths, and known client industries — not generic staffing firms.
