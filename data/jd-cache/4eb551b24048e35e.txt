 Ryan Burton
 Ryan Burton
 AI in Biopharma @ Kadence | AI & Machine Learning Talent Solutions
 Kadence is recruiting ML Researchers/Scientists in the healthcare space in the Bay Area.
 Frontier AI Systems | Small, High-Caliber Team
 We’re hiring exceptional ML researchers and engineers to work on large-scale models and real-world AI systems, from pretraining and scaling to post-training and deployment.
 What You’ll Do
 Train and scale foundation models (LLMs, multimodal, or domain-specific)
 Work on pretraining, optimization, and distributed systems
 Build post-training systems (fine-tuning, alignment, evaluation)
 Develop end-to-end ML systems with real-world feedback loops
 What We’re Looking For
 PhD (or equivalent) in ML, CS, Math, Physics, etc. from a top institution
 1 - 4+ years experience in ML research or applied AI
 Strong in deep learning (transformers, generative models)
 Experience in one or more of:
 large-scale pretraining
 distributed training / scaling
 post-training/alignment
 Strong coding skills (Python + C++ / Rust preferred)
 Proven ability to build and train models, not just apply them
 Ideal Candidate
 ML-first thinker with strong fundamentals
 Enjoys hard, real-world problems beyond benchmarks
 Thrives in small teams with high ownership
 Company compensating between $200,000 base to $400,000 base, depending on experience, before equity.
