 Lead Machine Learning Engineer (Enterprise Platforms Technology)
 Salary: £197,300 - £225,100 annual
 A leading financial services organisation known for its innovation in technology and data-driven decision-making is looking for a Lead Machine Learning Engineer (Enterprise Platforms Technology). This role focuses on the technical design, development, and implementation of production-scale machine learning applications within an Agile environment. You will lead architectural design and ensure the high availability and performance of critical machine learning systems.
 What you'll be doing
 • Design and deliver ML models and components to solve complex business problems in collaboration with Data Science teams.
 • Lead the development and testing of application code while automating deployment through cloud-based architectures.
 • Construct and maintain optimized data pipelines to feed machine learning models at scale.
 • Implement continuous integration and deployment best practices including test automation and production monitoring.
 • Ensure all code and models follow governance standards for risk management and Responsible AI practices.
 What we're looking for
 • Bachelor’s Degree and at least 6 years of experience building data-intensive solutions using distributed computing.
 • At least 4 years of professional programming experience with Python, Scala, or Java.
 • At least 2 years of experience building, scaling, and optimizing machine learning systems in a production environment.
 • Proven ability to collaborate within cross-functional teams to enhance big data and ML application software.
 • Must have the right to work in the United States and be eligible for required regulatory clearances.
 What's on offer
 • Competitive annual salary within a transparent pay structure.
 • Opportunity to work with emerging technology platforms and state-of-the-art ML frameworks.
 • A hybrid work environment that balances professional collaboration with flexibility.
 • Access to continuous learning initiatives focused on the latest innovations in machine learning engineering.
