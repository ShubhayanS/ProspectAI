 Data Analyst Intern (Remote – USA)
 FinTech Client | Immediate Start | Entry-Level Friendly
 Looking to kickstart your career in Data Analytics? Join our fast-growing FinTech client and work on real-world data, insights, and business problems in a dynamic environment.
 Role: Data Analyst Intern
 Location: Remote – United States
 Type: Internship (Full-time / Part-time)
 Compensation: Paid Internship (competitive, based on experience)
 What You’ll Work On
 Analyze large datasets to generate actionable insights
 Build dashboards using Excel, Google Sheets, Power BI, or Tableau
 Write basic SQL queries (training provided if needed)
 Support product, business, and risk teams with data requests
 Clean, organize, and structure data for decision-making
 What We’re Looking For
 Currently pursuing or recently completed a degree in STEM, Business, Economics, or Analytics
 Strong interest in data, analytics, and problem-solving
 Basic proficiency in Excel or Google Sheets
 Familiarity with SQL or Python (nice to have, not required)
 Strong communication skills
 Ability to work independently in a remote environment
 Perks & Benefits
 🌍 100% Remote (U.S.-based)
 📊 Hands-on experience with real FinTech data & projects
 👩 🏫 Mentorship from experienced data professionals
 📜 Internship Certificate + Letter of Recommendation
 🚀 Potential for extension or full-time conversion
 Who Should Apply
 Students and recent graduates across the U.S.
 Career switchers looking to enter data analytics
 Candidates seeking real-world, hands-on experience
 How to Apply
 Submit your application via LinkedIn Easy Apply. Shortlisted candidates will be contacted.
