 About the Role
 This opportunity is for a Senior Data Analyst supporting data-driven decision-making in a healthcare-focused environment. The role centers on using large-scale datasets to identify behavioral patterns, build product and client-facing analytics, and shape data initiatives that improve how teams understand performance and opportunities.
 This position works closely with product leaders, managers, analysts, and developers to move projects from data ingestion through analysis and final recommendations.
 What You’ll Do
 Use extensive datasets to identify and classify behavioral patterns among medical professionals on the platform
 Create both product-facing and client-facing analytics that support decision-making and performance visibility
 Help inform data team strategy by partnering with product leaders and managers
 Participate in execution and selected planning activities tied to organizational data strategy
 Collaborate with product managers, analysts, and developers to define and lead data projects from ingestion through analysis and recommendations
 Qualifications
 At least 4 years of professional experience as a data analyst or data scientist
 Advanced knowledge of statistical concepts, especially exploratory data analysis techniques and probability theory
 Some exposure to machine learning methods, including regressors and classifiers
 Advanced SQL skills with the ability to create and evaluate complex queries involving multiple tables and data relationships
 Proficiency in Python, including object-oriented programming concepts as they relate to data work
 Proficiency with distributed data processing concepts and execution, including experience with column stores, Spark, or similar tools
 Excellent visualization and data storytelling skills, with the ability to clearly explain results and solutions to stakeholders
 Ability to learn quickly, with strong curiosity and enthusiasm for working with data
 Tools & Technical Environment
 UNIX command-line interface and standard programming tools such as vim, emacs, git, and related developer workflows
 GitHub-based development environment with a large repository footprint
 Distributed team collaboration model
