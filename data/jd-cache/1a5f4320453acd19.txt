 SQL Data Analyst
 New Jersey - Remote
 6+ Months
 W2 role
 Job description
 We are looking for a highly analytical and detail-oriented SQL Data Analyst to support and enhance reporting, data validation, and reconciliation processes for a large-scale financial system.
 This role is not a typical developer role it requires deep expertise in SQL, advanced Excel, and data validation, with the ability to troubleshoot complex data mismatches across systems, reports, and dashboards.
 Key Responsibilities
 Data Analysis
 Develop, optimize, and maintain complex SQL queries
 Generate summarised and transaction-based reports
 Handle large datasets and ensure accurate aggregations and summaries
 Support business teams with ad-hoc data requirements
 Data Validation & Reconciliation
 Identify and resolve data mismatches between reports, database, and UI
 Perform end-to-end data validation across modules
 Conduct reconciliation of financial data across multiple sources
 Ensure data accuracy, consistency, and integrity
 SQL & Database Management
 Write complex queries involving joins across multiple tables
 Use CTEs, window functions, subqueries, and stored procedures
 Optimize queries for performance (indexing, execution plans)
 Debug and fix issues related to incorrect data outputs
 Issue Debugging & Support
 Investigate and resolve production issues related to reporting/data
 Analyze discrepancies reported by business teams and MLIs
 Coordinate with development teams for fixes and improvements
 Maintain logs and audit trails for issue resolution
 Required Skills (Must Have)
 Core Skills
 Strong expertise in SQL (Advanced Level)
 Excellent knowledge of data validation & reconciliation techniques
 Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel
 Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
 Must have Reporting experienc with Tableau
 ****Domain Experience****
 Experience in Financial Module (Accounts Ledger, Payable, Receivable)
 Exposure to financial data, transactions
 Understanding of data flows in enterprise applications
