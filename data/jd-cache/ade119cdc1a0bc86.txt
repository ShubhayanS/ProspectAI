 1. About Our Client:
 The organization operates in the edge cloud platform industry, enabling customers to create digital experiences that are fast, secure, and reliable by processing and serving applications close to end-users at the edge of the Internet. This approach supports agile software development and leverages the modern internet’s capabilities. Their customers include prominent companies across various sectors, highlighting the platform''s broad impact and reach.
 2. About the Opportunity:
 The Associate Business Data Analyst will serve as a strategic data partner within the global Business Analytics team, focusing on the Client Services organization. This high-autonomy, individual contributor role involves owning data architecture, visualization, and in-depth analysis that supports critical reporting and decision-making. The position enables the organization to optimize service delivery, enhance customer health and retention, and translate complex data into actionable business insights across global teams.
 3. Responsibilities:
 • Own tracking, reporting, and analysis of Client Services activities to drive customer health and retention
 • Write, optimize, and execute complex SQL queries to extract and analyze customer and business data
 • Translate non-technical business needs into tailored technical queries and dashboards for cross-functional partners
 • Collaborate asynchronously within a global team across significant time zone differences
 • Maintain and optimize data infrastructure, proactively identifying and fixing data pipeline issues
 • Develop automated frameworks and repeatable processes to improve team efficiency
 • Audit data outputs regularly to ensure accuracy and maintain leadership trust in metrics
 4. Requirements:
 • Significant experience in B2B business analytics, with expertise in Salesforce; experience with Gainsight or Zendesk is a plus
 • Advanced proficiency in SQL and experience with Google BigQuery and Looker
 • Deep understanding of customer lifecycle, retention, and account health metrics in SaaS or infrastructure environments
 • Experience building customer segments and target audiences from behavioral and financial data
 • Ability to build trusted relationships with global stakeholders independently
 • Strong communication skills to simplify complex data into clear business insights
 • Bachelor''s degree or equivalent practical experience in a highly analytical field
 Additional preferred qualifications:
 • Experience with dbt and version control systems like Git
 • Knowledge of statistical programming languages such as Python or R
 • Experience with Customer Success Platform implementation or advanced data management
 • Experience working in a global remote environment with large time zone differences
 5. Pay Range and Compensation Package:
 • The estimated salary range for this position is $83,220.00 to $117,492.00
 • Starting salary may vary based on experience, skills, qualifications, and location
 • This role may be eligible for equity and discretionary bonus programs
 6. Benefits & Perks:
 • Medical, dental, and vision insurance
 • Family planning and mental health support, including Employee Assistance Program
 • Life, disability, and accident insurance
 • Flexible vacation policy and up to 18 days of accrued paid sick leave
 • 401(k) plan with company match
 • Employee Stock Purchase Program
 • 12 paid local holidays and 12 paid company wellness days in 2026

All qualified applicants will receive consideration for employment without regard to race, color, religion, gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, or national origin.
