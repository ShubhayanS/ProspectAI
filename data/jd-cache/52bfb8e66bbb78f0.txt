 1. About Our Client:
 The organization serves the US federal government across sectors including defense, national security, public safety, civilian, and military health. It addresses complex challenges faced by these agencies through technology and innovation, supporting mission-critical operations. With a workforce of over 13,000 employees, the organization focuses on delivering solutions that enhance national security and public service effectiveness.
 2. About the Opportunity:
 The Data Engineer is responsible for designing, building, and maintaining scalable data pipelines and workflows that support analytics and reporting needs. This role ensures the integration of diverse data sources into unified datasets and implements processes to maintain data quality and reliability. By leveraging cloud services and collaborating with cross-functional teams, the position contributes to delivering data solutions aligned with business goals and federal mission priorities.
 3. Responsibilities:
 • Design, build, and maintain scalable end-to-end data pipelines using Databricks, Spark, and related technologies
 • Develop efficient data processing and transformation workflows
 • Integrate diverse data sources including APIs, databases, and cloud storage into unified datasets
 • Collaborate with data science, analytics, and business teams to design data solutions
 • Implement validation, monitoring, and observability processes to ensure data accuracy and reliability
 • Support data governance, security, and automation initiatives
 • Utilize AWS services such as S3, Glue, Lambda, and Redshift for cloud-native data solutions
 4. Requirements:
 • Experience with cloud-based ETL services (AWS Glue, Google Cloud Dataflow, Azure Data Factory)
 • Experience with cloud data warehousing technologies (Amazon Redshift, Google BigQuery, Snowflake)
 • Proficiency in Python, SQL, Spark, and PySpark
 • Experience with data platforms like Databricks, Palantir, and Snowflake
 • Familiarity with data orchestration and data quality processes
 • Must be a US Citizen
 Bonus qualifications:
 • Secret security clearance or higher
 • Experience with federal clients
 • Knowledge of COTS and open-source data engineering tools such as ElasticSearch and NiFi
 • Experience with Docker, Kubernetes, Hadoop, Spark, NiFi, ELK stack
 • Agile or Scrum experience
 • Relevant data engineering certifications (Palantir Foundry Data Engineer, Azure Data Engineer Associate, Google Professional Data Engineer, IBM Certified Data Engineer, or similar)
 5. Pay Range and Compensation Package:
 • The pay range for this role in specified locations (California, Colorado, Hawaii, Illinois, Maryland, Massachusetts, Minnesota, New Jersey, New York, Washington, Vermont, the District of Columbia, and the city of Cleveland) is $93,400 to $176,200 USD
 • Compensation varies based on location, role, skills, and experience
