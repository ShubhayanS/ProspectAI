 Srinivas Pavuluri
 Operations Manager at Wall Street IT Consulting Services (India) Priavte Limited
 Job Title: Databricks SME (Subject Matter Expert)
 Location : Jersey City NJ
 Duration :Fulltime
 Experience: 10+ Years
 Domain: Banking / Financial Services
 Job Summary
 We are seeking a highly experienced Databricks Subject Matter Expert (SME) to lead the design, development, and optimization of large-scale data platforms for Client. The ideal candidate will have deep expertise in Databricks, Spark, and cloud-based data engineering, along with strong experience in financial services data ecosystems, governance, and regulatory compliance.
 Key Responsibilities
 Act as a technical SME for Databricks platform architecture and implementation
 Design and build scalable data pipelines using Databricks, Apache Spark (PySpark/Scala)
 Lead migration of legacy data platforms to Databricks Lakehouse architecture
 Optimize performance of ETL/ELT pipelines, Delta tables, and Spark jobs
 Implement data governance, security, and compliance frameworks (PII, GDPR, banking regulations)
 Collaborate with stakeholders (Data Architects, Business, Risk teams) to define data strategies
 Provide best practices for CI/CD, DevOps, and DataOps in Databricks
 Troubleshoot complex production issues and ensure high availability & reliability
 Mentor team members and provide technical leadership and guidance
 Required Skills & Experience
 Core Technical Skills
 Strong expertise in Databricks (Workspace, Jobs, Notebooks, Delta Lake)
 Advanced experience in Apache Spark (PySpark/Scala)
 Hands-on experience with Delta Lake, Unity Catalog, and Lakehouse architecture
 Strong knowledge of ETL/ELT design and data pipeline optimization
 Experience with SQL, data modeling, and performance tuning
 Cloud Platforms (Preferred)
 AWS (S3, EMR, Glue, Lambda) OR
 Azure (ADLS, Synapse, ADF) OR
 GCP (BigQuery, Dataflow)
 Data Engineering & Integration
 Experience with Kafka / streaming pipelines
 Strong understanding of batch and real-time data processing
 Integration with tools like Informatica, Talend, or other ETL tools
 DevOps & Automation
 Experience with CI/CD pipelines (Jenkins, GitHub Actions, Azure DevOps)
 Infrastructure as Code (Terraform preferred)
 Version control using Git
 Domain & Compliance Experience
 Strong experience in Banking / Capital Markets (Citi preferred)
 Knowledge of:
 Data governance frameworks
 Regulatory compliance (GDPR, BCBS, CCAR, etc.)
 Data lineage and auditing
 Soft Skills
 Excellent communication & stakeholder management
 Ability to work in onsite-offshore model
 Strong problem-solving and leadership skills
 Good to Have
 Databricks certifications
 Experience with ML/AI workloads on Databricks
 Exposure to data cataloging tools (Collibra, Alation)
 Experience with GenAI or advanced analytics
