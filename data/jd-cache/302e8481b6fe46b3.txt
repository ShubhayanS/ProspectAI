 About The Company
 HelioCampus is a leading provider of data analytics solutions tailored specifically for higher education institutions. Dedicated to empowering colleges and universities with actionable insights, HelioCampus combines innovative technology with deep industry expertise to support strategic decision-making, improve operational efficiency, and enhance student success. With a commitment to advancing higher education through data-driven strategies, the company serves a diverse portfolio of clients across the United States, fostering a collaborative environment that values innovation, integrity, and continuous improvement.
 About The Role
 We are seeking a highly skilled Data Analyst to join our team of higher education data experts. In this role, you will work closely with multiple university partners to understand their analytical needs, evaluate key performance metrics, and analyze trends to support strategic initiatives. The ideal candidate will possess strong technical abilities, experience working directly with university clients, and a solid understanding of enrollment, student success, and financial data. Your primary responsibilities will include developing dashboards, reports, and visualizations to communicate insights effectively, conducting ad hoc analyses, and serving as a key liaison between clients and technical teams. This position offers the opportunity to influence higher education institutions' decision-making processes through data analysis and strategic support, contributing to the broader mission of improving student outcomes and institutional efficiency.
 Qualifications
 A minimum of 3 years of experience performing analytics in higher education or working as a higher education consultant
 Experience with key higher education areas such as student financials, financial aid, admissions, enrollment management, or student affairs
 Proven ability to summarize and present data analysis to business leaders, including making strategic recommendations
 Experience working with Student Information Systems (e.g., Banner, PeopleSoft, Colleague, Workday)
 Strong proficiency in Power BI and/or Tableau for data manipulation, analysis, and visualization
 Familiarity with data warehouse concepts, relational databases, and large data sets
 Foundational knowledge of SQL and experience with analytic software such as R, SAS, or Python
 Excellent communication and relationship management skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively with technical teams and clients
 Innate curiosity and a passion for advancing higher education through data-driven insights
 Responsibilities
 Develop subject matter expertise in the HelioCampus data platform and guide clients in its effective use for strategic and operational questions
 Design, deploy, and configure dashboards, reports, and visualizations to communicate insights clearly to university partners
 Analyze higher education data to identify trends, patterns, and opportunities for institutional improvement
 Perform ad hoc analyses to support strategic decision-making processes
 Serve as the primary point of contact between HelioCampus and client institutions, fostering strong relationships and ensuring client needs are met
 Provide ongoing training and support to client stakeholders to maximize platform utilization
 Translate business and data requirements between clients and technical teams to ensure clarity and accuracy
 Maintain data quality and accuracy in data extracts and reports
 Contribute to the creation of standardized dashboard templates, playbooks, and documentation for analysis deliverables
 Participate in process improvement initiatives aimed at streamlining delivery workflows
 Support Tableau development projects, including documentation and presentation of findings
 Benefits
 Competitive salary package
 Paid time off and holidays
 Comprehensive health insurance including medical, dental, and vision coverage
 401(k) plan with company match
 Parental leave policies
 Flexible remote work arrangements
 Home office setup and equipment perks
 Collaborative and innovative work environment

HelioCampus is an equal opportunity employer. We celebrate diversity and are committed to creating an inclusive environment for all employees.
