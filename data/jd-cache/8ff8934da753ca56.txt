 About The Company
 BLH Technologies, Inc. is a reputable provider of innovative technology solutions and consulting services dedicated to supporting government agencies and organizations in achieving their strategic objectives. With a focus on leveraging data-driven insights, BLH Technologies specializes in delivering customized solutions that enhance operational efficiency, improve program outcomes, and ensure compliance with federal regulations. The company prides itself on its commitment to excellence, integrity, and fostering long-term partnerships with clients across various sectors, including healthcare, education, and public administration.
 About The Role
 BLH Technologies, Inc. is seeking a detail-oriented and analytical Data Analyst to join our team supporting a federal contract with the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services (HHS). In this role, you will be instrumental in analyzing complex data sets related to multiple grant programs, helping to evaluate performance metrics, and generating comprehensive reports to inform decision-making processes. The ideal candidate will possess a strong background in federal reporting, data analysis, and visualization, with the ability to communicate insights effectively to both technical and non-technical stakeholders. This position offers an excellent opportunity to contribute to impactful government initiatives and advance your career within a collaborative and dynamic environment.
 Qualifications
 The successful candidate will possess a combination of education, skills, and experience, including:
 Proven experience in data collection, analysis, and reporting within a federal or grant-funded environment.
 Demonstrated ability to analyze program performance data and generate detailed reports or evaluations.
 Familiarity with federal reporting tools and systems such as Performance Progress Reports (PPR), OLDC, GrantSolutions, or similar platforms.
 Experience supporting or contributing to formal reports to Congress or other high-level government documentation.
 Strong quantitative and qualitative data analysis skills, with proficiency in relevant software tools.
 Exceptional problem-solving, critical thinking, and analytical capabilities.
 Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to present complex data insights clearly and concisely.
 Ability to collaborate effectively with federal staff, program teams, and cross-functional partners.
 Bachelor’s degree in Data Science, Statistics, Public Administration, or a related field; advanced degrees are a plus.
 Responsibilities
 The primary responsibilities of this role include, but are not limited to:
 Analyzing program and grant recipient data to evaluate performance, identify trends, and measure outcomes.
 Supporting the preparation of Reports to Congress and other key program performance reports, ensuring accuracy and compliance with federal standards.
 Assisting in data collection, validation, and quality assurance processes to maintain data integrity.
 Reviewing and analyzing recipient Performance Progress Reports (PPRs) and other reporting data to support program oversight.
 Developing data summaries, dashboards, and visualizations to facilitate effective program management and strategic decision-making.
 Conducting both quantitative and qualitative analyses to identify areas for program improvement and inform technical assistance strategies.
 Supporting Paperwork Reduction Act (PRA) clearance activities, including the development of data collection instruments and protocols.
 Collaborating with program staff and system specialists to ensure data systems meet program needs and are optimized for reporting requirements.
 Identifying program risks, gaps, and opportunities through comprehensive data analysis and reporting.
 Contributing to the development of reports, briefings, and written products that summarize findings and provide actionable recommendations.
 Benefits
 BLH Technologies, Inc. offers competitive benefits to support the well-being and professional growth of our employees. These include comprehensive health insurance plans, retirement savings options, paid time off, and professional development opportunities. Employees also benefit from a collaborative work environment that encourages innovation, continuous learning, and career advancement. Additionally, this role provides the chance to work on meaningful projects that have a tangible impact on public health initiatives and federal programs, fostering a sense of purpose and achievement.
