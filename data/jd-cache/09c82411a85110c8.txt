 Jobright is your personal AI job search agent that transforms the job search process into a fast, expert-guided journey. They are seeking an AI Engineer to build and scale business-facing AI agents, managing the entire lifecycle from prototype to production.
 Why Join Us
 Build real, production AI agents used by real users
 High ownership and impact
 Work at the intersection of AI, agents, and product
 Shape how people experience AI-driven job search
 Responsibilities
 Build and refine autonomous AI agents that interact with complex environments to solve real-world user problems
 Develop and optimize sophisticated prompt engineering workflows and multi-agent orchestration frameworks to enhance product capabilities
 Integrate Large Language Models (LLMs) into the Jobright application, ensuring high-quality, low-latency, and reliable AI responses
 Work at the intersection of product and engineering to transform user requirements into functional AI-driven features
 Implement Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems and fine-tune models to improve the accuracy and context-awareness of our agents
 Qualification
 Required
 Recent graduate or entry-level professional (0–2 years of experience) with a degree in Computer Science, AI, or a related technical field
 Strong proficiency in Python and experience working with LLM APIs (such as OpenAI, Anthropic, or open-source models via Hugging Face)
 Practical understanding of agentic workflows, prompt chaining, and the latest trends in generative AI
 Solid software engineering foundations, including a strong grasp of APIs, asynchronous programming, and version control (Git)
 Must live in and be authorized to work in the United States
 Preferred
 Previous internship or personal project experience building AI-powered applications, chatbots, or autonomous agents
 Familiarity with agent frameworks such as LangChain, AutoGPT, or CrewAI
 Experience with vector databases and semantic search implementation
 Basic understanding of front-end or full-stack integration to help bridge the gap between AI logic and user interfaces
