 JOB OPENING — Data Analyst (Entry Level) ADF Medical Services Inc.
 We're hiring a curious and motivated Entry Level Data Analyst to join our growing analytics team. If you have a passion for numbers, a keen eye for patterns, and a desire to make an impact in the healthcare industry — this is your opportunity to kickstart your data career!
 Role: Data Analyst (Entry Level)
 Department: Analytics & Business Intelligence
 Location: Remote / On-Site 🕐 Type: Full-Time / Part-Time
 Requirements
 What You'll Do:
 Assist in collecting, cleaning, and organizing healthcare and operational datasets
 Support the analytics team in building basic reports and data summaries
 Help maintain and update dashboards and performance tracking tools
 Identify basic trends and patterns in data to support team insights
 Prepare clear and simple data reports for internal teams and management
 Collaborate with senior analysts and department teams on data projects
 Ensure data accuracy, consistency, and security across all platforms
 Participate in paid training, onboarding, and ongoing mentorship sessions
 Benefits
 Competitive starting pay
 Paid training and mentorship from senior analysts
 Health, dental & vision benefits (full-time employees)
 Paid time off & paid holidays
 Flexible scheduling options
 Hands-on experience with real healthcare data
 Clear path for career growth within ADF Medical Services
 No experience needed — we will train you
