 Overview
 Ready to do meaningful work with an industry leader? We are seeking top talent to join our QA Team!
 Rollins, Inc. is seeking a highly analytical and detail-oriented Data Analyst – Operational Support to join our team in a newly created role. This position will play a critical role in enabling data-driven decision-making across Rollins’ Operational Support functions, including Quality Assurance, Claims, Technical Services, Training & Innovation, and Government Regulatory & Relations .
The ideal candidate brings strong analytical rigor, exceptional attention to detail, and the ability to translate complex operational data into clear, actionable insights for leaders and cross-functional partners. This role is well-suited for an analyst who enjoys partnering with business teams, identifying trends, and improving operational effectiveness through data.
While a member of the Operational Support team, this role will be integrated with our Enterprise Data & Analytics organization to ensure alignment and knowledge of Rollins data strategy, environments, standards and best practices. With this structure, the role will be well-positioned to grow both in business and operational knowledge while building data and analytical skills.
 Apply in minutes with a resume, even from your mobile!
 Responsibilities
 You will...
 Serve as a trusted analytical partner to Operational Support teams by providing data analysis, trend analysis, reporting, and insights that inform operational decisions
 Facilitate I nternal Communications and provide analysis and discussion on trends from operational data and other data sets.
 Interpret and translate trends and form recommendations to improve operational effectiveness, efficiency, and compliance
 Develop and maintain recurring and ad hoc reports, dashboards, and presentations for leadership and cross-functional stakeholders
 Collect, validate, and analyze data from multiple operational systems and data sources to identify trends, risks, and improvement opportunities
 Collaborate with business partners to define reporting requirements, KPIs, and success measures
 Ensure data accuracy, consistency, and documentation of assumptions and methodologies
 Continuously identify opportunities to improve reporting processes, analytics workflows, and data quality
 The Benefits . . .
 Challenging position with a financially stable and reputable company
 Comprehensive benefits package including medical, dental, vision, maternity & life insurance
 401(k) plan with company match, employee stock purchase plan
 Discounts, tuition reimbursement, and dependent scholarship awards
 Paid Time Off
 Work Environ ment: This is an inperson position at our Atlanta- Buckhead main campus @ 2170 Piedmont Rd NE, Atlanta, GA 30324, US.
 Why Rollins?
 Rollins, Inc., is a global consumer and commercial service company that provides accurate, comprehensive, and efficient pest management services for both residential and commercial customers. The Pest Management industry is $20B and growing and provides services and protection against termite damage, rodents, and insects to more than 2M customers in the US alone. Rollins is headquartered in Atlanta, GA, and has over 800 locations worldwide, 20,000 teammates, and reaches over $3B Revenues annually (NYSE: ROL).
The core business is operated through our wholly owned subsidiaries, the largest of which include Orkin US, HomeTeam Pest Defense, Clark Pest Control, Fox Pest Control, Orkin Canada, Western Pest Services, Northwest Exterminating, and TruTech Wildlife.
 The mission of Rollins is to empower our brands to focus on best serving their teammates and customers.
 Learn more about Rollins careers as well as our diversity, equity, and inclusion efforts on our careers page: www.careers.rollins.com
 Qualifications
 The Experience You Will Bring (Minimum Requirements):
 Minimum of three years proven experience or education in a data analysis and reporting role
 Bachelor’s degree in business, operations management, computer science, statistics, or related field
 Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to translate data into actionable insights
 Proficiency in data analysis tools, BI tools, data visualization techniques, and software, including advanced Excel functions (i.e., Microsoft Fabric, PowerBI, etc.)
 Strong proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite. Excellent organizational and communication skills
 Ability to work independently and collaboratively
 Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to present findings to both technical and non-technical audiences
 Ability to manage multiple priorities, work independently, and collaborate effectively in a fast-paced, team-oriented environment
 What Will Put You Ahead (Preferred Experience, Knowledge, Skills and Abilities):
 Ability to work with multiple groups
 Knowledge of the professional pest management industry
 Familiarity with Service Now platforms or similar workflow/service management platforms
 Experience collecting and analyzing detailed data to develop and present insights
 Demonstrated ability to synthesize large, detailed datasets into concise insights and recommendations
 Physical Demands / Working Environment:
 We require the ability to pass a drug screen and background checks. Candidates must have the ability to perform the requirements of the job with or without accommodations.
 This is an onsite position (4 to 5 days/week) at our Atlanta- Buckhead main campus @ 2170 Piedmont Rd NE, Atlanta, GA 30324, US
