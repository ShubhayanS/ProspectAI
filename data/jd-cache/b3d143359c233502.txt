 About The Company
 Judi Health is an innovative enterprise health technology company dedicated to transforming healthcare delivery through comprehensive digital solutions. We provide a suite of integrated platforms and services designed to streamline health benefit management for employers, third-party administrators (TPAs), and health plans. Our offerings include Capital Rx, a public benefit corporation delivering full-service pharmacy benefit management (PBM) solutions to self-insured employers, and Judi, our proprietary Enterprise Health Platform (EHP) that consolidates claim administration workflows into a secure, scalable system. Our mission is to rebuild trust in healthcare by deploying robust infrastructure that enhances transparency, efficiency, and patient outcomes. Committed to fostering a diverse and inclusive workplace, Judi Health values innovation, integrity, and collaboration.
 About The Role
 We are seeking a highly motivated and talented Data Engineer I (Analytics Engineering) to join our team and contribute to building and scaling Capital Rx’s analytics foundation. This role is pivotal in developing high-quality, analytics-ready datasets and establishing a consistent metrics and semantic layer that power reporting, self-service business intelligence, and operational insights. The successful candidate will work extensively with dbt and Snowflake to design durable data models, define trusted business metrics, and ensure data quality through testing, documentation, and observability practices. Collaborating closely with operational, product, and analytics stakeholders, you will translate complex business questions into reusable data products that enable data-driven decision making and measurable outcomes. This position offers an excellent opportunity to grow within a dynamic organization committed to innovation in healthcare technology.
 Qualifications
 Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience
 Strong SQL skills with experience building analytics datasets in a modern cloud data warehouse (preferably Snowflake)
 Hands-on experience with dbt core, including model development, tests, documentation, macros, and deployments
 Solid understanding of data modeling principles, including dimensional modeling, facts and dimensions, grain, and slowly changing dimensions (SCD)
 Experience in defining and managing metrics, translating business logic into KPI definitions, and maintaining metric governance
 At least 1 year of experience delivering production-grade analytics models, marts, or BI-ready datasets (internships or co-op experience may be considered)
 Excellent collaboration skills with the ability to communicate effectively with both technical and non-technical stakeholders
 Strong documentation skills and attention to detail
 Self-motivated with a problem-solving mindset and the ability to deliver high-quality work under tight deadlines
 Responsibilities
 Partner with operational and analytics teams to translate business needs into analytics-ready data models, curated data marts, and reusable datasets
 Build and maintain dbt projects, including staging, intermediate marts, model layering, macros, exposures, and source freshness monitoring
 Design and evolve dimensional models, including facts, dimensions, and SCD patterns, aligned with key business domains
 Define and own metrics end-to-end, establishing KPI logic, naming conventions, calculation rules, and governance standards
 Maintain a metrics and semantic layer to ensure consistent KPI definitions across tools and teams
 Implement data quality practices such as dbt tests, constraints, anomaly detection, and monitoring to ensure data reliability
 Develop comprehensive documentation and enablement assets, including dbt docs, data lineage, data dictionaries, and onboarding guides
 Optimize data warehouse performance and cost-efficiency through SQL tuning, incremental models, and appropriate data design choices in Snowflake
 Adopt disciplined software engineering practices including Git workflows, peer reviews, CI/CD checks, version control, and environment promotion
 Proactively identify and resolve data issues, ensuring system reliability and data integrity
 Provide ongoing support for critical analytics data products, participating in on-call rotations as needed
 Adhere to Capital Rx’s Code of Conduct, including reporting any non-compliance issues
 Benefits
 Competitive salary within the range of $100,000 to $120,000 USD, commensurate with experience and skills
 Remote work flexibility within the United States
 Comprehensive health, dental, and vision insurance plans
 Generous paid time off and holidays
 Professional development opportunities and continuous learning support
 Inclusive and diverse workplace culture that values innovation and collaboration
 Participation in company initiatives aimed at improving healthcare infrastructure
