 Role Title: Data Analyst I – Branch Operations & Inventory Analytics
 Experience: 0–3 years
 Project Tenure: 18 Months
 Work Mode: Hybrid (4 days onsite, 1 day remote)
 Work Location: McKinney, Texas / (Remote)
 Employment Type: Full-Time
 Core Responsibilities
 Perform daily and weekly inventory reconciliation across multiple ERP systems (Prophet 21, Eclipse) to identify stock mismatches and posting delays
 Analyze branch-level sales vs. inventory movement to flag dead stock, overstock, and stock-out risks
 Build SQL queries to extract and validate transactional data (orders, receipts, transfers) from internal databases
 Support standardization of SKU mapping across acquired branches with inconsistent item master structures
 Work with branch managers to validate discrepancies between system data and physical counts
 Generate operational reports tracking fill rates, backorders, and inventory turnover at branch level
 Identify patterns in demand variability and recommend adjustments to reorder points and safety stock
 Assist in data cleanup initiatives tied to ERP consolidation efforts
 Participate in weekly operations calls with regional leadership to review analytics findings
 Required Qualifications
 Bachelor’s degree in Data Analytics, Supply Chain, Business, or related field
 0–3 years of experience working with structured business or operational datasets
 Strong SQL skills (multi-table joins, aggregations, filtering large datasets) — mandatory and tested
 Hands-on Excel experience (pivot tables, lookups, data cleaning) in a professional or internship setting
 Ability to work with transactional data (sales, inventory, logistics, or operations)
 Comfortable working in a fast-paced, branch-driven operational environment
 Willingness to work onsite and interact with non-technical stakeholders
 Preferred Qualifications
 Exposure to inventory, warehouse, or distribution datasets
 Familiarity with ERP systems like Prophet 21, Eclipse, SAP, or similar
 Experience supporting operations or supply chain teams
 Basic understanding of demand planning, stock movement, or fulfillment metrics
 Experience with Power BI or Tableau (supporting role, not primary)
