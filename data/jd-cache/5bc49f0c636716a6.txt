 This position is posted by Jobgether on behalf of a partner company. We are currently looking for an Associate, Operations Data Analytics in United States.
 This role sits at the intersection of analytics and healthcare operations, focused on turning complex data into clear, actionable insights that improve business performance and member outcomes. You will work closely with cross-functional stakeholders to identify inefficiencies, monitor key operational and financial metrics, and support data-driven decision-making across business units. Acting as a trusted analytics partner, you will independently scope and deliver analyses that shape strategic and operational initiatives. The environment is fast-paced, mission-driven, and highly collaborative, with a strong emphasis on measurable impact and continuous improvement. You will also help translate data findings into compelling narratives for both technical and non-technical audiences. This position offers the opportunity to influence healthcare operations at scale while working within a modern, technology-enabled organization.
 Accountabilities
 Own insights generation and ongoing analysis for key business units, acting as a primary analytics partner for senior stakeholders and leadership teams.
 Analyze large and complex datasets to identify trends, inefficiencies, and opportunities for operational and financial improvement.
 Translate data findings into clear, compelling stories and presentations tailored for non-technical audiences and executive decision-making.
 Define requirements and improvements for dashboards, reporting tools, and performance monitoring systems as business needs evolve.
 Develop analytical models and tools to surface insights that improve healthcare efficiency and support strategic initiatives.
 Collaborate cross-functionally to track initiative impact, align on metrics, and support broader strategic and operational projects.
 Ensure compliance with relevant regulations and contribute to additional assignments as business needs require.
 Requirements
 3+ years of experience in data analytics, reporting, or similar technical roles involving structured data analysis and insights generation.
 Strong experience working with large datasets and translating complex analysis into structured, actionable business insights.
 2+ years of SQL experience with ability to write queries involving filtering, aggregation, and CTEs.
 2+ years of experience building data models and supporting analytical frameworks.
 Proficiency in Excel or Google Sheets, including advanced functions such as VLOOKUPs, nested IF statements, and linked datasets.
 Experience building dashboards using tools such as Looker or other BI/visualization platforms.
 Strong communication skills with demonstrated experience presenting analytical findings to senior stakeholders (Director level and above).
 Bonus: experience with Python or R, healthcare/insurance industry exposure, dbt, Looker Studio, Git workflows, or member/customer operations environments.
 Benefits
 Competitive base salary range: $91,659 - $120,303 per year.
 Eligibility for annual performance bonuses.
 Full healthcare coverage including medical, dental, and vision plans.
 Unlimited paid time off program and paid sick leave.
 Paid parental leave and family support benefits.
 401(k) retirement savings plan participation.
 Life and disability insurance coverage.
 Wellness time and reimbursement programs.
 Fully remote work flexibility with potential future hybrid options depending on business needs.
