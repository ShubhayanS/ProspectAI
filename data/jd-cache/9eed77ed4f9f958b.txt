 Company Insight:
 A leading global investment firm is expanding its sector-focused data science capability, embedding data more deeply into the investment research process. This team partners directly with investors to generate differentiated insights by applying rigorous analytics to large, complex, and often unstructured datasets.
 This is a high-impact role for someone who enjoys turning raw data into clear, decision-ready insight — and who wants their work to directly influence how capital is allocated. You’ll operate as a thought partner to investment teams, bringing structure, speed, and analytical rigour to how data informs research and portfolio construction
 The Role:
 Partner with investment teams to understand their research approach and identify how data can strengthen decision-making
 Analyse data from multiple structured and unstructured sources to test complex investment theses
 Rapidly assess data relevance, detect emerging trends, and separate signal from noise
 Identify the key operational and fundamental drivers across companies and sectors
 Deliver high-quality research outputs in a clear, concise format tailored for senior investors
 Help build and evolve scalable datasets, analytical frameworks, and decision-support tools
 Experience/Skills Required:
 Strong intellectual curiosity with a genuine interest in markets and investing
 Ability to quickly digest fundamental research and translate narratives into data-driven analysis
 Advanced Python skills (including Pandas) and strong SQL
 Solid background in statistical modelling and quantitative analysis
 Experience working with large, complex, or unstructured datasets
 Prior exposure to investment management, equity research, or buy-side analytics
 Bachelor’s degree in statistics, engineering, economics, computer science, physics, or a related discipline

 Please do not apply if you are looking for a contract or remote work
