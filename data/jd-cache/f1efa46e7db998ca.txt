 Job Title : Business Intelligence Intern
 Location : Wisconsin, United States
 Job Type : Full Time
 Our Client is looking for a motivated Business Intelligence professional to join a centralized BI team operating under a shared‑services model. This role focuses on designing, building, and supporting enterprise‑wide analytics solutions, including data pipelines, data marts, and business intelligence products that serve multiple business domains.
 As part of the BI team, you will collaborate closely with fellow analysts, software development teams, and BI product owners to deliver scalable, reliable, and insight‑driven analytical solutions.
 Functional Areas Supported
 Logistics Analytics
 Develop analytics solutions that provide visibility into customer orders, transportation activity, and warehouse operations. Partner with application and product teams to interpret data that helps improve service levels, manage freight costs, optimize inventory flow, and enhance warehouse efficiency.
 Shared Services Analytics
 Create and maintain enterprise BI solutions supporting Accounting, Human Resources, and Supply Chain functions. Deliver governed data models, reporting, and dashboards that promote consistent, trusted insights across corporate operations.
 Operations Analytics
 Enable data‑driven decision‑making across manufacturing and production operations. Translate production and operational data into actionable insights supporting capacity planning, equipment performance, scheduling, and raw material usage.
 Sales & Marketing Analytics
 Design BI solutions that support sales, estimating, and marketing activities. Integrate transactional and customer data to provide visibility into sales performance, pricing trends, order execution, and customer engagement.
 Key Responsibilities
 Design, develop, test, and debug business intelligence solutions across the enterprise analytics platform
 Contribute to the development and support of ELT/ETL pipelines, data marts, enterprise data warehouses, and end‑user reporting solutions
 Work collaboratively within an agile team to enhance analytics products serving both operational and administrative functions
 Partner with software engineers and BI product owners to deliver high‑quality, scalable data solutions
 Technology Stack (Experience or Exposure Preferred)
 Programming & Querying: Python, SQL, VB
 Databases & Platforms: Microsoft SQL Server, Oracle
 BI & Reporting Tools: Tableau Server, Power BI, SSAS, SSRS
 Data Integration: Informatica PowerCenter, Informatica Intelligent Cloud Services (IICS), Alteryx
 Qualifications
 Currently pursuing or recently completed a Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, MIS, Data Analytics, Data Science, or a related discipline
 Authorized to work in the United States without sponsorship
 Willingness and ability to work onsite at Wisconsin manufacturing locations
 Desired Skills & Attributes
 Strong analytical and problem‑solving capabilities
 Solid understanding of relational databases and data modeling
 Familiarity with data integration and BI visualization concepts
 Enthusiasm for software development and data analytics
 Positive, collaborative mindset with a strong desire to learn
