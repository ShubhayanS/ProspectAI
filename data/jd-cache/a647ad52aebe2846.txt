 Company Overview
 For over 25 years, Edgesource Corporation has served as an innovative technology service provider for the Department of Defense (DOD), Department of Homeland Security (DHS), Department of State (DOS), the U.S. Intelligence Community, Law Enforcement, and other federal, state, and commercial clients locally, nationally, and abroad. From providing boutique technical solutions in support of the DOD Counter Unmanned Aerial Systems (CUAS) mission set to addressing the most critical Cybersecurity threats facing our nation as a prime contractor with the DHS Cybersecurity & Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA), a career at Edgesource is an opportunity to do meaningful, interesting, and impactful work.
 Position Overview
 We are seeking a highly skilled and experienced data engineer to join our team. The ideal candidate will have experience working with large-scale enterprise level data systems on cloud-based architecture platform, to include at a minimum integration of large data, protection of large data, and migration of large data.
 Job Duties
 Design, develop, and maintain large-scale enterprise-level data systems using cloud-based architectures
 Develop data integration solutions for a variety of data sources and formats
 Ensure the integration of data from multiple sources, including internal and external systems, APIs, and third-party tools
 Develop and implement data protection strategies, including data encryption, access controls, and disaster recovery plans
 Manage and execute data migration projects, ensuring data integrity and accuracy during the migration process
 Collaborate with cross-functional teams to define and execute data-related projects and initiatives
 Perform data profiling, data cleansing, and data transformation tasks to ensure data quality and consistency
 Develop and maintain documentation related to data architecture and data flows
 Implement best practices for data management, including data modeling and data quality assurance
 Continuously improve the efficiency, scalability, and reliability of data systems and processes
 Stay up-to-date with industry trends and emerging technologies related to data engineering
 Required Qualifications
 Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Engineering or related field
 Active TS/SCI clearance
 Minimum of 5 years of experience in data engineering with a focus on large-scale enterprise level data systems
 Proficient in cloud-based data technologies, such as AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud Platform
 Demonstrated expertise in designing and building large scale enterprise-level data systems
 Proven ability to integrate data from multiple sources and formats
 Strong understanding of data protection and security best practices
 Experience with data modeling, ETL processes, and data quality assurance
 Excellent problem-solving skills and ability to work independently and as part of a team
 Nice to Have Qualifications
 Experience working in cloud environments (e.g., AWS GovCloud, Azure Government).
 Experience implementing data quality checks, monitoring pipelines, and optimizing performance for large-scale datasets.
 Working at Edgesource
 As an ISO 9001:2015 certified and CMMI Level 3 appraised small business, Edgesource specializes in providing a variety of technical solutions to include software development, database services, enterprise networking, data center virtualization, and management support.
