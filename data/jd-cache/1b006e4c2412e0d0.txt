 Client Location: Charlotte, NC
 Job Description
 Total years of data engineering experience 5 to 7 years with relevant GCP experience of 1years
Experience in Data Engineering ETL Pipelines Batch and Streaming along withSQL Python PySpark UnixLinux and Data Warehousing concepts
Experience in GCP native services like BigQuery Composer GCS Dataflow etc
Experience in Data Migration along with Data Quality and Data Governance
Experience of working in an Agile Scrum KANBAN Environment
Participate proactively in standup meetings scrum ceremonies grooming sessions team retrospectives and suggest improvements
Articulate and facilitate conversations with business owners and delivery teams
Detailed understanding of Continuous Integration and Continuous Deployment Test Automation and Deployments
Document and prioritize data and pipeline defects working closely with defect management teams for resolution
Status reporting Project Risk Issue Management Business stakeholder reviews approvals of deliverables
