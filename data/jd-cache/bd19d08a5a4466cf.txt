 NextPath Workforce Solutions is currently seeking a Applied AI Engineer to join our client’s team in Dunwoody, GA . This is a onsite, direct hire position.
 SALARY: $150,000–$160,000 (depending on experience)
Unfortunately, at this time our client is unable to sponsor or transfer visas. Only candidates authorized to work in the US without sponsorship should apply.
 Requirements
 Experience developing LLM-powered applications, RAG pipelines, AI agents, ML models, and production Python services
 Familiarity with Agent Bricks, Amazon Bedrock AgentCore, Claude Code, and modern data platforms
 Strong Python skills, including API development and data/ML libraries
 Experience with cloud platforms, preferably AWS
 Knowledge of Databricks environment and related tools
 Responsibilities
 Build and deploy AI/ML and LLM-powered applications in production
 Design and evaluate AI agents and workflows for logistics use cases
 Develop RAG pipelines, prompt workflows, and automation solutions
 Create predictive models for ETA, anomaly detection, and shipment intelligence
 Develop production-grade Python services and APIs for AI integration
 Collaborate with teams on AWS infrastructure for model deployment and scaling
 Contribute to CI/CD pipelines, observability, and MLOps best practices
