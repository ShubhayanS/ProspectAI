 Job Requirements
 6–12 years of experience in software development, with a focus on AI and machine learning.
 Proficiency in AWS services, including Amazon SageMaker and Amazon Bedrock.
 Strong programming skills in Python, Java, or other relevant languages.

 Preferred

 Knowledge of data engineering and ETL processes.
 Experience with containerization technologies such as Docker and Kubernetes.
 Understanding of data privacy and security best practices.
