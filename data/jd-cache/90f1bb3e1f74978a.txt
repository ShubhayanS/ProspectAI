 About The Company
 Transcarent is a forward-thinking healthcare company dedicated to transforming the way healthcare services are delivered and experienced. By leveraging innovative data-driven solutions, Transcarent aims to improve health outcomes, enhance patient engagement, and streamline clinical operations. The organization fosters a collaborative environment where technological advancements meet clinical expertise, ensuring that members and clients receive personalized, efficient, and high-quality care. Committed to diversity and inclusion, Transcarent values the unique perspectives of its employees and strives to create an inclusive workplace that promotes growth, learning, and excellence.
 About The Role
 The Data Analyst – Care Delivery is a vital individual contributor role responsible for converting complex operational and healthcare data into actionable insights through reports and self-service dashboards. Reporting directly to the Director of Clinical Insights, this role serves as the analytical backbone supporting care delivery reporting, clinical stratification, engagement channel performance, and population health strategies. The Data Analyst collaborates closely with Care Delivery Nursing, Quality, and Health Impact leaders to design and implement sophisticated analyses that facilitate data-driven decision-making across the organization. The role demands a strong analytical mindset, technical proficiency, and the ability to communicate insights effectively to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
 Qualifications
 Bachelor’s degree in Statistics, Mathematics, Health Informatics, Computer Science, Business, or a related quantitative field; Master’s degree preferred
 Minimum of 5 years of progressive experience in data or business analytics, with exposure to healthcare operations, clinical data, or population health environments
 Expert-level proficiency in Tableau for designing interactive, scalable dashboards
 Advanced SQL scripting skills (T-SQL, PL/SQL) for navigating complex relational schemas and multi-source datasets
 Hands-on experience with cloud-based data warehouses such as Amazon Redshift; familiarity with Big Data environments like Hadoop/Hive and Databricks architecture
 Applied experience with predictive modeling, cohort matching, statistical analysis, or machine learning frameworks in healthcare or operational contexts
 Proficiency in Excel for rapid prototyping and ad-hoc modeling; Python knowledge is a plus
 Experience in UAT/QA processes to ensure high-quality reporting and dashboards
 Working knowledge of medical claims data structures, clinical performance metrics, and operational drivers of healthcare costs and utilization
 Understanding of population health concepts including care gap identification, risk stratification, and engagement channel performance measurement
 Strong analytical, problem-solving, and pattern recognition skills
 Excellent stakeholder communication skills with the ability to translate technical findings into clear narratives
 Self-starter with a proactive approach and the ability to manage multiple workstreams independently
 Effective collaborator capable of working within a matrixed organization and managing competing priorities
 High attention to detail and commitment to data accuracy and integrity
 Responsibilities
 Design, develop, and maintain operational dashboards and reports in Tableau that provide real-time insights into clinical efficiency and operational success metrics
 Collaborate with clinical leaders to define, validate, and evolve key performance indicators (KPIs) that reflect organizational priorities
 Respond to strategic analytical questions related to staffing models, resource capacity, and engagement channel effectiveness
 Build and automate processes for clinical quality standards aligned with audit requirements, utilizing advanced SQL queries across complex datasets
 Synthesize findings into clear, concise narratives and develop dashboards to support self-service analytics for clinical and operational teams
 Support the refinement and validation of predictive models used for member targeting and clinical program design
 Assess model effectiveness, recommend improvements, and respond to leadership questions regarding performance guarantee attainment
 Analyze member behavior, claims history, and engagement patterns to support clinical stratification and population segmentation
 Partner with cross-functional teams including Product, Technology, and Clinical leaders to ensure data assets are accurate and support operational goals
 Monitor clinical performance guarantees, identify trends and anomalies, and support remediation planning
 Document data workflows, metric definitions, and analytical methodologies to ensure organizational knowledge transfer and audit readiness
 Benefits
 Competitive medical, dental, and vision insurance coverage
 401(k) plan with a generous company match
 Flexible Paid Time Off (PTO) and 13 paid holidays annually
 Protection plans including Life Insurance, Disability Insurance, and Supplemental Insurance options
 Mental health and wellness programs to support employee well-being
