 About The Role
 The Lead Machine Learning Engineer at Capital One plays a pivotal role in designing, developing, and deploying scalable machine learning solutions that address complex business challenges. As part of an agile, cross-functional team, you will be responsible for architecting ML systems, building robust models, and ensuring their seamless integration into production environments. This role offers an exciting opportunity to work on cutting-edge technologies, influence architectural decisions, and lead initiatives that improve decision-making processes across various domains. You will collaborate closely with data scientists, data engineers, product managers, and operations teams to deliver innovative solutions that drive business growth and customer satisfaction.
 Qualifications
 To be successful in this role, candidates should possess a strong educational background, typically a Bachelor's Degree in computer science, electrical engineering, mathematics, or related fields. A minimum of six years of experience in designing and building data-intensive distributed solutions is required, along with at least four years of programming expertise in Python, Scala, or Java. Additionally, candidates should have a minimum of two years of experience in building, scaling, and optimizing machine learning systems. Preferred qualifications include advanced degrees such as a Master’s or Doctorate, extensive experience with industry-recognized ML frameworks like TensorFlow, PyTorch, or Spark, and a proven track record of developing production-ready data pipelines and deploying ML solutions in cloud environments such as AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud Platform. Leadership experience and contributions to the industry through publications, open source projects, or patents are also highly valued.
 Responsibilities
 Design, develop, and deploy machine learning models and components that solve real-world business problems, working collaboratively with product and data science teams.
 Make informed decisions regarding ML infrastructure, including model selection, data and feature engineering, hyperparameter tuning, and validation techniques.
 Write, test, and maintain application code to automate model training, testing, deployment, and monitoring processes.
 Participate in agile development cycles, contributing to the creation and enhancement of scalable software solutions for big data and ML applications.
 Maintain and monitor models in production, ensuring high availability, performance, and reliability.
 Leverage cloud-based architectures and technologies to optimize ML model deployment at scale.
 Construct and optimize data pipelines to facilitate efficient feeding of data into ML models.
 Implement continuous integration and deployment practices, including automated testing and model monitoring, to ensure seamless updates and performance.
 Ensure all code adheres to security best practices, models are governed from a risk perspective, and ethical AI principles are followed, including responsible and explainable AI.
 Utilize programming languages such as Python, Golang, and others to develop robust ML solutions.
