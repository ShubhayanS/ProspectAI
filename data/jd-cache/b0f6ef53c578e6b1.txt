 Cloud experience (AWS/GCP/Azure) with modern data stack exposure

 Ability to work directly with front office stakeholders
 Why This Role
 Direct impact on alpha generation (not just “data janitor” work)
 Exposure to cutting-edge research and trading strategies
 Lean team, high ownership
