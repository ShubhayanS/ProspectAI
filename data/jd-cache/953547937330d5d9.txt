 Mary Quinif
 Mary Quinif
 Technical Recruiter at firstPRO 360
 A growing and innovative company is seeking a Data Scientist to join a high-impact team focused on building advanced analytics and machine learning solutions. In this role, you will design and optimize forecasting models, develop AI/LLM-driven solutions, and help translate complex business problems into scalable, production-ready data products. You will partner closely with engineering, product, and client-facing team. This is a hands-on role ideal for someone who enjoys working with real world data challenges in a fast-paced environment. The company offers competitive compensation, strong benefits, and a flexible, trust-based hybrid work culture.
 Requirements:
 5+ years of experience in data science, ML, or analytics
 Strong Python skills with extensive experience using Data Science (Pandas, etc.)
 Expert-level SQL abilities and experience working with complex and imperfect datasets
 Familiar with metric design and experience deploying and monitoring forecasting models
 Hands-on experience delivering data science solutions that drive business outcomes
 Ability to work cross-functionally and with non-technical stakeholders
