 About The Company
 Field AI is a pioneering company at the forefront of robotic embodied AI technology, dedicated to transforming various industries such as construction, security, mining, and manufacturing. Our mission is to develop risk-aware, reliable, and field-ready AI systems that address complex real-world challenges in robotics. With a focus on pushing the boundaries of traditional data-driven approaches and transformer-based architectures, we are charting a new course in AI development. Our solutions are already deployed globally, delivering tangible results and continuously improving through real-field applications. Our team comprises experts from leading organizations including DeepMind, NASA JPL, Boston Dynamics, NVIDIA, Tesla, and SpaceX, bringing decades of experience in autonomous systems, perception, and machine learning. Headquartered in Irvine, California, with a global presence, we foster an innovative and inclusive environment that encourages collaboration and excellence.
 About The Role
 We are seeking a highly skilled 3D Machine Learning Engineer to join our dynamic team. In this role, you will focus on designing, implementing, training, and maintaining advanced 3D and multimodal machine learning models that process reality capture data such as 3D point clouds, 360-degree photos, and RGBD images. Your work will be pivotal in automating progress tracking, deviation analysis, and semantic scene understanding within construction sites and other complex environments. The role requires close collaboration with software, autonomy, and product teams to ensure seamless integration of AI models into our production systems. You will contribute to developing scalable pipelines, optimizing deep learning models, and working with diverse sensor inputs to enhance our autonomous robotic solutions. This position offers an exciting opportunity to impact the future of embodied AI and robotics technology through innovative research and practical deployment.
 Qualifications
 Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Machine Learning, Robotics, or a related technical field
 Minimum of 2+ years of industry experience developing and deploying machine learning systems for 3D perception, point clouds, or spatial understanding tasks
 Strong background in 3D machine learning, deep learning for point clouds, multi-view fusion, or geometric learning
 Proficiency in Python and deep learning frameworks such as PyTorch, TensorFlow, or equivalent
 Experience with classical computer vision and 3D data preprocessing using OpenCV and PCL (Point Cloud Library)
 Hands-on experience with training, evaluating, and deploying ML models on cloud platforms like AWS, SageMaker, or similar, including containerized workflows
 Solid understanding of the end-to-end ML lifecycle, including experiment tracking, reproducibility, and model versioning
 Ability to work effectively within interdisciplinary teams across software, ML, and product domains
 Responsibilities
 Design and implement scalable machine learning pipelines for processing large-scale 3D spatial data, including point cloud analysis, object detection, segmentation, and scene understanding
 Train, optimize, and deploy deep learning models using frameworks such as PyTorch or TensorFlow on cloud infrastructure
 Collaborate with software and systems engineers to integrate models into production environments and enhance inference pipelines
 Analyze sensor inputs from various modalities, including RGBD imagery, LiDAR, 360 photos, and Building Information Models (BIM)
 Work closely with data annotation and labeling teams to develop robust data strategies that ensure high model performance and generalization
 Continuously evaluate and improve model accuracy, robustness, and efficiency for deployment in real-world scenarios
 Stay updated with the latest research in 3D perception, geometric deep learning, and related fields to incorporate innovative techniques into projects
 Benefits
 Competitive salary range between $70,000 and $300,000 annually, commensurate with experience and location
 Flexible work arrangements including hybrid and remote options
 Comprehensive health, dental, and vision insurance plans
 Generous paid time off and leave policies
 Opportunities for professional growth and development in a cutting-edge technological environment
 Collaborative and inclusive company culture that values diversity and innovation
 Participation in impactful projects that shape the future of robotics and AI technology
