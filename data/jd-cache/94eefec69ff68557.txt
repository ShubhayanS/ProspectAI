 Mohammed Fazal Ur Rahman
 Mohammed Fazal Ur Rahman
 We are Hiring Java Devs, Data Professionals
 Job Description: Power BI Engineer (Analytics Engineer)
 Role Overview
 We are looking for a Power BI Engineer who can bridge the gap between raw data and actionable business insights. You will be responsible for transforming raw data into models that users can consume and enabling self-service reporting across our key business segments and corporate functions.
 Key Responsibilities
 Build new enhancements on the platform, ranging from adding new fields to semantic models to ingesting entirely new subject areas of data.
 Transform raw data into actionable formats using DBT.
 Build out Kimball-based star schemas and pull them into Power BI semantic models.
 Manage the process of getting data ingested onto the model and building out the final reporting solution.
 Work tickets and troubleshoot issues with users to ensure products are delivered effectively, though the primary focus remains on enhancements.
 Utilize Microsoft Purview as the data cataloging and management tool sitting on top of the platform.
 Technical Stack & Skills
 Snowflake.
 Azure Data Factory for raw ingestion and Click Replicate for CDC-based data pulling.
 DBT.
 Power BI (Semantic Models and Star Schemas).
 Building and managing orchestration pipelines.
 Ability to perform raw ingestion using APIs, understanding full loads versus deltas, and familiarity with CI/CD for Power BI.
