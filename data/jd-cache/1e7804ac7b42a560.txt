 Senior Revenue Analyst | Hybrid
 We're hiring on behalf of a Haystack partner!
 The Role
 • Analyze client revenues and P&L drivers to ensure accuracy and provide insights.
 • Partner with Finance and Operations teams to deliver meaningful revenue analysis.
 • Own monthly revenue forecasts for specified branches using the revenue system.
 • Collaborate with sales operations business partners to manage forecast adjustments.
 • Monitor actual revenue posting during month-end close to ensure accuracy.
 • Assist with M&A transitions and provide revenue forecasting and reporting for new acquisitions.
 What You'll Need
 • Bachelor's degree with 4 years of related experience.
 • Strong Excel skills and experience with financial management software.
 • Ability to form relationships and collaborate across finance and business functions.
 • Good written and oral communication skills, with the ability to present insights to executives.
 • Analytical skills to understand financial planning and analysis.
 • Basic understanding of IT systems and data flows.
 What's On Offer
 • Competitive compensation and comprehensive benefits package.
 • Medical, dental, and vision plans starting from day one.
 • 401(K) and Roth options, plus tax-advantaged accounts.
 • Opportunities for professional development and training.
