 A Brief Overview
 The Benefits Data Analyst supports the Client Open Enrollment Team and is an integral team player responsible for performing data management retrieval, reporting and processing functions supporting the company's multifaceted open enrollment processes, practices, and system. An extensive portion of this position's time is spent gathering data from a variety of sources, compiling results and reporting on findings. The development of reports involves perceptive comparative analysis and manipulation of data. Reports are expected to include insightful recommendations based on assessment within the broader context of the incumbent's experience in and/or knowledge of the health care and health benefits industry.
 What You Will Do
 Analyze client performance, producing reports and reconciling large amounts of disparate data. This includes analyzing and interpreting claims, fees, and conditions data to determine present and future financial performance.
 Create and manage the client costing tool.
 Understand multiple data sources and structures to maintain strong data infrastructure, bringing relevant data sources into a consolidated environment for more efficient data manipulation and relationship reporting.
 Create queries using design techniques that ensure adherence to database development standards using a variety of database applications.
 Coordinate with all levels of management to gather, analyze, and summarize data to accommodate all stakeholder requests.
 Assist with the annual client renewal process for medical, dental, and other healthcare benefits.
 Document business requirements to ensure systems and processes are set up correctly.
 Responsible for carrier rate validation.
 Performs other duties as assigned
 Complies with all policies and standards
 Education Qualifications
 Bachelor's Degree or equivalent experience required
 Bachelor's Degree in Finance, Economics, or other business-related areas preferred
 Experience Qualifications
 Typically 2+ years working with relational databases and complex spreadsheets required
 Experience in healthcare analytics and/or insurance preferred
 Skills And Abilities
 Ability to critically evaluate information gathered from multiple sources, reconcile any identified conflicts, and categorize high-level information
 Strong Microsoft Office experience. Advanced proficiency in Excel required
 A demonstrated commitment to high professional ethical standards and a diverse workplace
 Ability to adapt to a fast paced continually changing business and work environment while managing multiple priorities
 Comfortable across multiple systems and interfaces. Experience with data warehouse preferred.
 Strong critical thinking, problem solving, and analytical skills with an attention to detail.
 Ability to work independently and meet deadlines
 Knowledge of medical utilization terminology and analysis preferred
 Excellent verbal and written communication skills. Ability to communicate with colleagues at all levels of the organization
 Travel Requirements
 Travel not required
 Work Environment
 Work in clean, pleasant, and comfortable office or home setting.
