 About the job Senior Business Data Analyst
 Job Description
 We are looking for a Data Steward. The Senior Business Data Analyst will have an end to end experience in creating Strategic Ad-Hoc analytics report. Develop effective business intelligence strategies and analytics solutions. Ideal candidate will have a background in analytics, focusing on SQL and Tableau. Sales Operations knowledge is a plus.
 What you get to do in this role:
 Generate Ad-Hoc Strategic reports and present these to senior management
 Execute analytics projects to create basic dashboards on Tableau environment, this also includes ability to write basic SQL
 Design and create wireframes for data visualizations on Tableau as part of project requirements discussion
 Define critical KPIs and build core operating dashboards to help teams drive operational rigor and manage performance
 Solve and present complex problems in an understandable way to stakeholders, business leaders, and executives'
 Create a UAT testing process for C&P org, and ensure smooth execution with a diverse array of cross-functional stakeholders
 Enforce and follow all company policies and procedures to ensure data quality and prevent discrepancies
 Identify business opportunities and ensure customer satisfaction
 Generate insights and storylines through translation of metrics and a deep understanding of our internal processes, partners and customers
 Qualifications
 To be successful in this role you have:
 Bachelor's degree is with Computer Science, Analytics is preferred
 Master's degree in a similar field is a plus
 Expert knowledge of Tableau, SQL and PowerBI (or similar analytics tools)
 Excel knowledge is a must
 Must be able to work in fast paced environment and be able to adopt to changing requirements
 Understanding of technology development projects and the full technology development life cycle
 Excellent communication and interpersonal skills
