 On-Site | 4 Days in Office + Fridays Remote
 We're looking for a hands-on Data Analyst with 3-5 years of experience to join our growing BI team. You'll build and maintain Power BI dashboards, write complex SQL across our data warehouse, and develop automated pipelines that turn ERP data into actionable insights. You'll collaborate with global stakeholders across the US, Israel, Mexico, and Europe to support KPI reporting, master data governance, and AI-driven initiatives.
 What will you do?
 Collaborate with cross-functional teams globally to translate business requirements into data solutions
 Contribute to AI and advanced analytics initiatives
 Create and maintain technical and business-facing documentation
 Write and maintain SQL queries, stored procedures, and views across a medallion architecture (Bronze/Silver/Gold) on SQL Server
 Build and optimize Power BI dashboards and data models, including DAX measures and Power Query transformations
 Support ETL pipeline development and maintenance using Azure Data Factory
 Perform data analysis across sales, operations, procurement, and engineering to surface trends and support strategic decisions
 What are we looking for?
 Exceptional communicator who asks the right questions, translating complex data into clear business insights and driving meaningful conversations across teams
 Bachelor's degree in Statistics, Mathematics, Computer Science, Information Systems, or related field
 3-5 years of hands-on T-SQL experience (CTEs, stored procedures, performance tuning)
 Proficiency in Power BI: data modeling, DAX, Power Query, and report design
 Familiarity with data warehouse concepts (dimensional modeling, ETL, layered architectures)
 Experience with automotive aftermarket master data (fitment data, parts interchange, catalog architecture) is a strong plus
 Background in industrial manufacturing (production, BOMs, work orders) preferred
 Self-motivated and comfortable managing priorities in a fast-paced global environment
