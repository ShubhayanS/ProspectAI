 About Catalyze Labs
 Catalyze Labs is an AI-native systems and product builder. We partner with enterprises to design and deploy production AI systems that sit inside real operations; often messy, regulated, and deeply human.
 Our work spans healthcare, financial services, logistics, and enterprise operations. We don’t sell tools or experiments. We build end-to-end systems that combine AI, automation, software, and people, and we stay involved until they work in the real world.
 Our client counterparts vary widely depending on the organization and problem: CTOs, COOs, product leaders, engineers, operators, and functional VPs. What they have in common is responsibility for outcomes.
 The Role
 We’re looking for a strong Data Engineer / Analytics Data Architect to jump into a fast-moving environment and help bring structure to a complex, multi-source data ecosystem .
 This isn’t a “greenfield build” or a slow-moving enterprise role.
 This is about quickly understanding what exists, connecting the dots, and making the data usable for both analytics and emerging AI workflows.
 The Opportunity
 You’ll work directly with a small, senior team to:
 Make sense of fragmented data across systems (data warehouse, BI tools, operational platforms)
 Map business questions and dashboards back to underlying data logic
 Design a lightweight semantic layer to standardize metrics and improve consistency
 Enable data to be used not just for dashboards—but for AI-driven workflows and querying
 Identify data quality issues and recommend practical, non-overengineered improvements
 What We’re Looking For
 Strong hands-on experience with Snowflake and SQL
 Background in analytics engineering / data modeling
 Comfortable working in messy, ambiguous data environments
 Ability to trace logic across views, stored procedures, and multiple systems
 Experience with BI tools (especially Power BI or similar)
 Someone who can bridge the gap between raw data → business metrics → usable insights
 Why This Role Is Interesting
 High ownership and direct impact
 Blend of data engineering, analytics, and AI enablement
 Opportunity to shape how data is structured for next-generation use cases
 Fast-paced, low-bureaucracy environment
 Logistics
 Contract role (near-term, with potential extension)
 Immediate start preferred
 Tight collaboration with a senior team
