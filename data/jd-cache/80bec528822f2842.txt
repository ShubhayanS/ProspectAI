 Role Overview
 AI/BI Analytics Engineer advances our in-home BI ecosystem. In this role, you will transform high‑volume data from systems such as Zoho, Acculynx, Vena, Acumatica, and various APIs into reliable, scalable models that support reporting across the business.
 You will maintain and enhance our reporting environment, ensuring trusted data models and high‑performing BI solutions. AI is central to how we build at IHS - this role develops primarily through AI tools (Claude, Gemini, OpenAI) , deploying frequently and iterating quickly. While AI accelerates delivery, you are fully accountable for architecture, validation, and quality.
 The ideal candidate brings exceptionally strong SQL, Elixir, JavaScript, and C# skills, a solid understanding of modern data engineering and medallion‑architecture patterns, and the ability to partner closely with stakeholders. This is a fast‑moving, collaborative role suited to an expert level AI operator writing, refining, and testing code at speed and delivering high‑quality data products.
 Key Responsibilities
 Own, refine, and maintain business‑ready datasets across all data layers (bronze → gold), ensuring accuracy, usability, and long‑term reliability.
 Develop, optimize, and document SQL transformations that standardize, consolidate, and model data from multiple systems.
 Partner closely with senior leadership (Director of FP&A, CFO, CEO) to translate business questions into trustworthy reporting and analytical outputs.
 Build and maintain the core logic that powers our next‑generation, web‑based reporting platform.
 Deliver new reports, dashboards, and data products with consistent definitions, shared metrics, and clear business logic.
 Manage and improve orchestration, scheduling, and monitoring of data pipelines, extraction processes, and transformation workflows.
 Strengthen data quality through rigorous validation, testing, documentation, and continuous QA improvements.
 Optimize SQL code and data models for performance, scalability, and maintainability within a growing analytics ecosystem.
 Support and contribute to the migration from legacy BI tools toward a modern, flexible reporting environment.
 Work collaboratively with technical and non‑technical teams to prioritize requests, troubleshoot issues, and enhance BI delivery processes.
 Leverage AI tools (e.g., Claude, Gemini, OpenAI) as the primary development workflow—writing, refactoring, and testing code in rapid, iterative cycles.
 IHS Job Description and Posting
 Requirements
 5+ years of experience in analytics engineering, BI engineering, data warehousing, or equivalent analytical development roles.
 Advanced SQL expertise, including writing, optimizing, and maintaining complex transformations and data models.
 Strong understanding of layered analytical architectures (bronze/silver/gold).
 Demonstrated ability to convert raw operational data into trusted reporting models and well‑structured layers.
 Strong communication and interpersonal skills with proven success supporting senior executives and cross‑functional partners.
 Experience administering, building, or supporting BI tools, reporting systems, dashboards, or custom reporting applications.
 Solid grasp of data quality practices, including testing, documentation, and long‑term production support.
 Ability to balance hands‑on technical execution with business‑facing ownership and delivery.
 Hands‑on experience using AI tools as the primary method of writing, debugging, and refining code—comfortable owning the output and validating accuracy.
 Genuine interest in emerging AI capabilities and a desire to apply them to accelerate BI development.
 A proactive, iterative work style with comfort deploying frequently, gathering feedback, and improving quickly.
 Benefits
 Opportunities for professional growth and development tied to your personal interests
 A transparent, supportive, and collaborative work environment
 The chance to make a positive impact on our communities and the environment
 Competitive: Health, dental, and vision coverage; PTO; Paid Holidays; Life Insurance; 401k with match
 Compensation
 This position offers up to $120,000 per year depending upon a variety of factors. This position offers additional performance-based compensation targeted to 10% of the base.
 About Us
 Infinity Home Services (“IHS”) was founded by Wisconsin entrepreneurs with a simple but ambitious goal: to raise the standard of residential roofing and exterior remodeling and “Save our Communities from Unscrupulous Contractors.”
 Today, we are recognized as a leader in our industry delivering exceptional roofing, windows, and siding installation services using only the most trusted, top‑quality materials.
 Our platform brings together a family of respected local brands, empowering each to enhance service delivery, strengthen community presence, and expand market share. We stand behind every job we perform, using top quality materials, best‑in‑class installation practices, and an ongoing commitment to do the right thing.
 IHS is rapidly expanding both organically and through strategic acquisitions. As we shape the future of the Home Services industry, we seek ethical, forward‑thinking professionals who bring innovation, integrity, and craftsmanship to their roles. The work we perform directly protects our customers’ most important investment, their homes!
 IHS is an equal opportunity employer. All applicants will be considered for employment without attention to race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, veteran or disability status.
