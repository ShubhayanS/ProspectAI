 Job Description - Data Engineer
 About the Company
YO IT CONSULTING is a forward-thinking technology solutions provider dedicated to delivering innovative and scalable IT services to clients across various industries. With a focus on leveraging cutting-edge technologies, YO IT CONSULTING strives to empower organizations through digital transformation, data-driven insights, and advanced AI solutions. Our team comprises skilled professionals committed to excellence, collaboration, and continuous learning, ensuring that our clients stay ahead in a rapidly evolving technological landscape.
 About The Role
 We are seeking an experienced Data Engineer to join our dynamic team on a contractual basis. In this role, you will be instrumental in developing and maintaining sophisticated data pipelines that support the training and optimization of next-generation AI agents. This fully remote position offers an exciting opportunity to work on innovative AI projects, where your technical expertise will directly influence the success of cutting-edge AI systems. You will collaborate closely with AI and machine learning engineers, contributing to the design and implementation of scalable data architectures that facilitate efficient data flow and high-quality training datasets.
 Qualifications
 The ideal candidate will possess a strong educational background in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related discipline, complemented by practical experience in data engineering within AI/ML environments. A minimum of three years’ hands-on experience is required, demonstrating proficiency in developing and managing data pipelines for complex projects. Candidates should have advanced skills in Python, MySQL, PostgreSQL, Java, and JavaScript, along with a solid understanding of AI concepts and agent training. Excellent communication skills are essential to articulate technical processes clearly to diverse stakeholders. Self-motivation, attention to detail, and the ability to thrive in a remote, collaborative setting are key attributes for success in this role.
 Responsibilities
 Design, implement, and manage data pipelines to facilitate AI agent training initiatives, ensuring scalability and efficiency.
 Collaborate with AI/ML engineers to optimize data flows and ensure the integrity of training datasets.
 Develop and maintain scalable ETL processes using Python, MySQL, and PostgreSQL to support large-scale data operations.
 Utilize Java and JavaScript to integrate diverse data sources and enable seamless data exchange across systems.
 Contribute to the enhancement of AI models by providing high-quality, well-structured data inputs.
 Document data processes comprehensively, ensuring clarity and ease of understanding for technical and non-technical teams.
 Troubleshoot and resolve data-related issues promptly, maintaining data integrity and reliability for AI applications.
 Benefits
 Joining YO IT CONSULTING as a Data Engineer offers numerous benefits, including the opportunity to work on innovative AI projects in a flexible remote environment. You will have access to continuous learning and professional development opportunities, collaborating with a talented team of professionals dedicated to technological excellence. Competitive compensation and a supportive work culture that values diversity, inclusion, and work-life balance are integral parts of our employment philosophy. Additionally, you will gain exposure to the latest AI and data engineering tools, enhancing your skill set and career trajectory.
Equal Opportunity
YO IT CONSULTING is an equal opportunity employer. We celebrate diversity and are committed to creating an inclusive environment for all employees. We do not discriminate based on race, ethnicity, gender, age, sexual orientation, disability, or any other protected characteristic. We believe that diverse perspectives and experiences drive innovation and excellence, and we are dedicated to fostering a workplace where everyone feels valued and empowered to contribute their best.
