The Organization:
Just Capital is on a mission to demonstrate how just business – defined by the priorities of the public – is better business. We believe that business and markets can and must be a force for the greater good and that by leveraging the resources of the $21.6 trillion private sector, we can drive competition to build a better future for all.
Just Capital engages directly with leaders of the largest corporations in the world, is supported by some of the country's premier foundations, and has forged an array of partnerships with leading institutions. The organization was co-founded by Paul Tudor Jones II, is chaired by Verizon CEO Dan Schulman, and is led by a high-profile Board.
The Opportunity:
We are seeking a Junior Data Analyst to support the continued growth, integrity, and impact of Just Capital’s data. This role will be instrumental in strengthening the organization’s ability to produce high-quality, trusted data on corporate behavior and performance across 1,000 of the largest U.S. companies. Reporting to the Senior Manager of Data Operations & Analytics, the Junior Data Analyst will contribute to the full data lifecycle, from data collection and validation to analysis and visualization, helping to ensure our data remains accurate, consistent, and actionable.
Working closely with colleagues across research, data, and technology teams, this role will support the maintenance and enhancement of internal datasets and data tools, review company-submitted feedback, and help translate complex data into meaningful insights. The Junior Data Analyst will also play a key role in advancing data standards, improving data quality processes, and identifying opportunities to scale impact through better use of data.
This is an ideal opportunity for an early-career professional who is detail-oriented, analytically curious, and motivated to apply data skills in service of a mission-driven organization. The role offers hands-on experience with real-world datasets, cross-functional collaboration, and the chance to contribute to innovative data products and insights that inform stakeholders across the business, policy, and investment communities.
Expectations & Responsibilities:
Be a key player in elevating the use of and confidence in best-in-class Just data:
 Collect data from publicly available sources on specific topics related to just corporate behaviors and activities for 1,000 companies
 Conduct data quality assurance checks throughout the data lifecycle, ensuring accuracy and consistency
 Support the maintenance and management of datasets and metadata across various internal data tools, including AI-driven tools. This includes updating records, monitoring data quality, and assisting with routine data operations to ensure accuracy and consistency
 Review and assess company-submitted feedback on datasets throughout the year, verify accuracy, and update internal systems and data products as needed
 Prepare summary statistics, analysis, and data visualization in support of other teams (including liaising with the tech team)
 Coordinate across teams’ data requirements and use cases; identify and empower sources of leverage to drive scale in pursuing Just Capital's mission
 Partner with other teams to enhance the data platform and provide analytical support; help find ways for data to have an impact
 Support with generating insights from user behavior data, forming the basis for new product features
 Help establish data standards for analytical data over business terms, business definitions, enterprise code sets, data elements, and business rules
 Leverage tools like SQL and Python for effective data querying, analysis, and visualization
 Knowledge, Skills & Abilities:
 Approximately 1–3 years of relevant experience in data analysis, research, or a related field; a strong interest in corporate behavior or mission-driven work aligned with Just Capital’s goals is a plus
 Proficiency in data analysis tools such as SQL and Python, with experience in querying, cleaning, and transforming datasets
 Strong attention to detail and commitment to data quality, with experience conducting QA checks and ensuring accuracy and consistency across datasets
 Experience working with structured datasets and metadata, including maintaining, updating, and documenting data assets
 Analytical mindset with the ability to synthesize large datasets into summary statistics, insights, and compelling narratives for diverse stakeholders
 Strong organizational and project management skills, with the ability to manage multiple priorities, meet deadlines, and operate effectively in a fast-paced environment
 Effective written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to collaborate cross-functionally and explain technical concepts to non-technical audiences
 Demonstrated ability to work collaboratively across teams, identify opportunities to improve processes, and contribute to scalable data solutions
 Curiosity and initiative to explore new data sources, tools, and methods, including leveraging user behavior data to inform product and analytical improvements
 Understanding of data governance concepts such as data standards, definitions, and business rules is a plus
 Location: New York, NY – Four days a week in the office
 Compensation: $65,000 to $72,000 annually
