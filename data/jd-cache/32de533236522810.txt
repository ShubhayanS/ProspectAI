 Meet Us
 Context Analytics, as a part of BridgeWise, is a leading provider of alternative data and sentiment analytics for financial markets. The firm leverages insights derived from social media, news, and other unstructured data sources to help institutional investors generate alpha, manage risk, and enhance investment decision-making across asset classes.
 The Role
 We are seeking a highly analytical and detail-oriented Quantitative Data Analyst to support research, product development, and client engagement initiatives at Context Analytics. This role sits at the intersection of financial markets, alternative data, and quantitative research, with a strong focus on leveraging sentiment data to generate actionable investment insights.
 How You’ll Make an Impact
 Research & Development
 Apply sentiment analytics and alternative data signals to enhance financial models and alpha generation
 Collaborate with research and engineering teams to design, test, and implement new data-driven factors and strategies
 Conduct exploratory data analysis on social media and alternative datasets to identify predictive signals across asset classes, including equities, FX, commodities, and ETFs
 Product & Data Support
 Prepare, validate, and manage datasets for internal use and client delivery
 Support product enhancements by translating client feedback into data-driven improvements
 Support product team on new POC (proof-of-concept) product ideas
 Client & Sales Enablement
 Support institutional clients in developing and refining alpha models using sentiment data
 Assist in managing client trials, including data delivery, performance analysis, and follow-ups
 Contribute to the development of marketing collateral, case studies, and research reports
 What You’ve Accomplished
 Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Mathematics, Economics, Computer Science, Data Science, or a related field
 For non-Finance majors, a strong working understanding of financial markets and asset classes; Prior experience in the Finance field is preferred
 What You’re Great At
 Proficiency in Python or R for data analysis and statistical modeling
 Working knowledge of SQL and database management
 Familiarity with financial modeling and quantitative analysis techniques
 Exposure to alternative data (e.g., social media, sentiment data) is a strong plus
 Basic understanding of machine learning techniques is desirable
 What You Excel At
 Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with keen attention to detail, and ability to learn quickly in fast-paced environments
 Ability to communicate complex data insights clearly to both technical and non-technical audiences
 Strong organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment
 Collaborative mindset with the ability to work cross-functionally
 Bonus Points If You Have
 Experience working with financial time-series data
 Familiarity with APIs and data pipelines
 Prior exposure to sentiment analysis or natural language processing
 What We Will Give You
 Competitive compensation and 401(k) matching with life insurance
 Generous time off and flexible leave policies
 Comprehensive medical, dental, and vision coverage
 Collaborative, transparent, and open-door office culture
