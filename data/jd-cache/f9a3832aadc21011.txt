 Data Analyst - Wealth Management Location: Austin, TX
 🎯 Role Purpose
The Data Analyst will support the Wealth Management team by leveraging data to provide insights into client portfolios, investment performance, and market trends. The role involves analyzing large datasets, building dashboards, and delivering actionable intelligence to advisors and senior management to enhance client outcomes and business efficiency.
🔑 Key Responsibilities
 Collect, clean, and analyze financial and client data from multiple sources
 Develop dashboards and reports to track portfolio performance, asset allocation, and risk metrics
 Provide insights into client behavior, investment trends, and product performance
 Support advisors with data-driven recommendations for client portfolios
 Collaborate with investment teams to evaluate new products and strategies
 Ensure compliance with regulatory requirements in data handling and reporting
 Automate recurring reporting processes to improve efficiency
 Present findings to stakeholders in clear, concise formats (visualizations, presentations, reports)
 📊 Required Skills & Competencies
 Strong proficiency in SQL, Excel, and data visualization tools (Power BI, Tableau, or similar)
 Knowledge of financial markets, investment products, and portfolio management concepts
 Experience with Python/R for statistical analysis and data modeling
 Ability to interpret complex datasets and communicate insights effectively
 Strong attention to detail and problem-solving skills
 Familiarity with regulatory frameworks in wealth management (e.g., KYC, AML, SEBI/SEC guidelines)
 🎓 Qualifications
 Bachelor's/Master's degree in Finance, Economics, Statistics, Data Science, or related field
 2-5 years of experience in data analysis, preferably within financial services or wealth management
 Professional certifications (CFA, FRM, or Data Analytics certifications) are a plus
