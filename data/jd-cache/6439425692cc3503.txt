 Javier Agosto
 Javier Agosto
 Founding Partner @ StellarMettle Placements | Entrepreneur | Career Coach | IT Enthusiast
 StellarMettle Placements is looking for an Analytics Engineer to build scalable, high-quality data models that power Sales and Revenue insights. This role sits at the intersection of data engineering and business intelligence, partnering closely with Sales Ops and GTM leadership to deliver trusted, actionable analytics.
 RESPONSIBILITIES:
 Design and build performant analytical datasets using advanced SQL and modern modeling practices
 Develop and maintain data models in DBT, including testing, documentation, and lineage
 Optimize data workflows within Snowflake, balancing performance and cost efficiency
 Translate Sales/Revenue business requirements into scalable data products and dashboards
 Support CI/CD-driven analytics development (version control, code reviews, deployment pipelines)
 Deliver executive-ready reporting for pipeline, forecasting, quota attainment, and revenue performance
 REQUIRED EXPERIENCE:
 5+ years in Analytics Engineering, Data Engineering, or advanced BI within a modern data stack
 Advanced SQL expertise with strong data modeling fundamentals (star/snowflake schemas, fact/dimension design, incremental models, medallion architecture)
 Hands-on experience with DBT (or equivalent transformation frameworks)
 Experience with Snowflake or similar cloud data warehouses (performance tuning, cost optimization)
 Proven ability to partner with business stakeholders and deliver trusted analytical assets
 Experience working in CI/CD-enabled data environments
 Experience supporting Sales Ops, RevOps, or Commercial Analytics teams
 Experience modeling CRM data (e.g., Salesforce)
