Data Scientist
 Why This is an Exciting Role:
 The Boeing Defense, Space & Security (BDS) organization, Intelligence Systems portfolio, is seeking a DevOps Developer to join our Mission Operations team in Herndon, VA. Our team focuses on delivering complex solutions in some of the most challenging environments on Earth. We provide support for our Mission Operations Portfolio area which design, build, test, deploy, operate, and maintain a wide variety of mission critical platforms and systems for
 commercial and government customers.
 This Data Scientist position performs data analytics development and data visualization to meet business requirements and provide data to the engineering teams to decrease risk and ensure engineering excellence.
 Developing data analytics capabilities in Python to solve business problems
 Developing automated processes/scripts to capture and analyze data to track Key Performance Indicators across a large-scale distributed system
 Assisting with system designs to enable data science capabilities
 Assisting with maintaining data quality
 Analyzing system performance, identifying bottlenecks, and suggesting optimizations to improve overall system efficiency and responsiveness
 Maintaining accurate documentation of system configurations, procedures, and troubleshooting steps
 Participating in the planning and implementation of new systems, upgrades, and migrations, ensuring compatibility and adherence to best
 practices
 Staying up-to-date with the latest technologies, industry trends, and security practices, and continuously improving skills and knowledge
