 Job Description:
 At GDIT, people are our differentiator. We are seeking a Operations Data Analyst to support the Member Services function of a federal limited healthcare program. Your work will strengthen the operational performance of a program serving a critical mission.
The World Trade Center Health Program is a limited federal health program administered by the National Institute for Occupational Safety and Health, part of the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention in the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services. The Program provides no-cost medical monitoring and treatment for certified WTC-related health conditions to those directly affected by the 9/11 attacks in New York, the Pentagon, and in Shanksville, Pennsylvania.
 HOW YOU WILL MAKE AN IMPACT:
 Analyze operational data, metrics, and performance indicators to identify trends, process gaps, and opportunities for improvement
 Manage the data flow process between GDIT and trading partners to make sure that all benefit assignments and supporting information are administered and updated correctly
 Research and respond to internal and external data requests, including Customer, clinic, and data center inquiries
 Conduct root‑cause analysis on data inconsistencies, workflow bottlenecks, and issues affecting member experience or program performance
 Translate business needs into data requirements, reporting specifications, and actionable operational insights
 Perform data extractions, validations, reconciliations, and quality checks specific to enrollment, certification, transfer, appeals, and demographic updates with trading partners
 Support forecasting, modeling, and scenario analysis aligned with program growth and resource planning
 Assist in the development and monitoring of program performance dashboards, operational scorecards, and contract deliverables
 Lead or support special analytics and process improvement projects for Member Services
 Minimum Qualifications
 WHAT YOU’LL NEED TO SUCCEED:
 BA/BS or equivalent, 2+ years of experience
 Strong analytical, critical thinking, and problem‑solving skills
 Proficiency with data analysis tools such as Excel, SQL, and Power BI
 Ability to interpret complex operational data and communicate insights clearly to diverse stakeholders
 Must have an understanding of operational processes, workflow optimization, performance metrics, and health program data
 Excellent written and verbal communication skills
 Strong attention to detail, accuracy, and consistency
 Ability to work independently and collaboratively in a fast‑paced, mission‑driven environment
 Ability to obtain and maintain a Public Trust clearance
 PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS:
 Experience supporting federal health programs such as CDC, CMS, VA, or similar environments
 Familiarity with the structure and operations of health plans or other Third‑Party Administrator (TPA) contracts
