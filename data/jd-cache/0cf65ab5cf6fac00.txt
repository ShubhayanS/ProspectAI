 Company Description
Chui Data & Analytics is a boutique consultancy specializing in Microsoft Fabric, Power BI, and modern cloud data platforms. We help organizations build end-to-end analytics solutions from raw data ingestion to executive dashboards with a focus on reliability, performance, and scalability. Our expertise lies in managing complex, multi-source environments and implementing robust architectures, such as medallion frameworks and data governance controls. As a founder-led company, we bring a hands-on approach to our work, designing solutions with long-term maintainability in mind. Our mission is to empower businesses with scalable, high-quality data systems tailored to their needs.
 Role Description
This is a full-time on-site role for an MS Fabric Data Engineer based in Cincinnati, OH. The MS Fabric Data Engineer will be responsible for designing, building, and maintaining data pipelines and analytics solutions. Day-to-day tasks include data engineering, creating and optimizing data models, implementing Extract, Transform, Load (ETL) processes, and managing data warehouse solutions. The engineer will collaborate with teams to implement scalable data platforms and ensure solutions adhere to best practices in data governance and quality control.
 Qualifications
 Proficiency in Data Engineering, including building and optimizing data pipelines
 Experience with Data Modeling and working with medallion architectures
 Strong skills in designing and managing Extract, Transform, Load (ETL) processes
 Knowledge of Data Warehousing principles and modern cloud-based platforms
 Understanding of Data Analytics to ensure actionable insights from curated models
 Strong troubleshooting and problem-solving skills with an eye for detail
 Familiarity with Microsoft Fabric and Power BI is highly desirable
 Bachelor's degree or relevant experience in Computer Science, Data Science, or a related field
 Excellent communication and collaboration abilities to work effectively with diverse teams
 Commitment to developing scalable, reliable, and maintainable solutions
