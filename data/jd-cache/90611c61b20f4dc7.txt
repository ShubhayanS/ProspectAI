 About The Company
 Virtualitics, Inc. is a pioneering leader in AI-native readiness applications tailored for defense, government, and critical infrastructure sectors. With a foundation rooted in over a decade of cutting-edge research conducted at Caltech in collaboration with NASA/JPL, the company is driven by a team of scientists, strategists, and servicemembers united by a shared mission: to address and solve the world’s most complex, mission-critical challenges through advanced artificial intelligence solutions.
Our innovative Readiness AI solutions are designed to deliver operational certainty by providing clear, actionable insights into system readiness, potential risks, and necessary interventions. By leveraging transparent and explainable AI, we enable organizations to identify risks early, diagnose root causes effectively, and prioritize actions to enhance operational efficiency and security. Our commitment to relentless innovation is reflected in our portfolio of over 15 U.S. patents and our leadership in agent-driven readiness technology. At Virtualitics, we foster a culture grounded in results, transparency, and compassion for the mission and the people we serve.
 About The Role
 We are seeking a highly skilled Machine Learning Engineer to join our dynamic team in the US, specifically within the DC Metropolitan Area. This role requires an individual with a strong technical background and the ability to obtain a U.S. government security clearance (TS/SCI). The successful candidate will play a pivotal role in enhancing our AI platform by integrating advanced machine learning models, optimizing data workflows, and developing scalable applications that support critical decision-making processes.
The Machine Learning Engineer will work closely with cross-functional teams, including data scientists, software developers, and product managers, to spearhead platform upgrades, develop dynamic dashboards, and improve overall system performance. This position offers significant career growth opportunities, including pathways to senior engineering roles and technical leadership positions, as we continue to push the boundaries of AI technology for national security and infrastructure resilience.
 Qualifications
 The ideal candidate will possess a degree in Computer Science or a related field, or have at least four years of relevant software engineering experience. A valid TS/SCI security clearance is mandatory, along with a willingness to travel and work from a Sensitive Compartmented Information Facility (SCIF) as required. Proven experience deploying software into production environments is essential, demonstrating a solid understanding of software development lifecycle best practices.
Proficiency in Python and familiarity with the Python Data Stack (including pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn, PyTorch, and Matplotlib) are required. Experience with big data technologies such as Spark, Databricks, and Snowflake is highly desirable. Knowledge of containerization and orchestration tools like Docker and Kubernetes, along with version control systems such as Git, is expected. Exceptional problem-solving skills, a strong sense of ownership, and excellent communication skills in English are crucial for success in this role.
Preferred qualifications include experience in end-to-end AI application lifecycle management, predictive maintenance, supply chain optimization, and cybersecurity monitoring. Familiarity with task schedulers like Celery, Airflow, or Prefect, as well as web development frameworks such as Flask or Django, or app-building tools like Streamlit or Plotly Dash, will be considered advantageous.
 Responsibilities
 Spearhead platform upgrades to ensure our AI solutions remain at the cutting edge of innovation and effectiveness.
 Design, develop, and manage dynamic dashboards utilizing the Virtualitics AI Platform Python SDK, transforming complex data into intuitive visualizations that support strategic decision-making.
 Optimize data access patterns and workflows to improve the efficiency, scalability, and responsiveness of our AI applications.
 Address runtime performance issues proactively, ensuring high responsiveness, stability, and reliability of deployed applications.
 Architect and implement robust, scalable, and user-friendly AI-driven applications, considering current technological trends and future growth potential.
 Collaborate with Technical Product Managers and cross-disciplinary teams to enhance usability, functionality, and overall user experience of our AI platform.
 Participate in code reviews, testing, and documentation to maintain high-quality standards across all deliverables.
 Benefits
 Competitive salary, equity options, and performance-based bonuses aligned with experience and qualifications.
 Comprehensive benefits package including medical, dental, and vision coverage to support employee well-being.
 Unlimited paid time off to promote work-life balance and personal development.
 Opportunities for professional growth through challenging projects, training, and leadership development programs.
 Work in a collaborative environment that values innovation, transparency, and impact-driven results.
