 Senior Machine Learning Engineer — 3D Reconstruction
 About the role
 We're hiring a Senior Computer Vision Engineer to lead our 3D work — reconstruction, neural rendering, and the production systems that bring them to life. You'll work with real-world sensor data, modern techniques like Gaussian Splatting, and have real scope to shape both our research direction and the systems we ship.
 This is a senior, hands-on role for someone who's lived inside 3D systems before — ideally at a self-driving car company, robotics startup, or another team pushing 3D reconstruction into the real world. If you're equally comfortable reading a neural rendering paper and shipping production code, you'll feel at home here.
 What you'll do
 Design and build 3D reconstruction pipelines from real-world sensor data
 Work with neural splatting (Gaussian Splatting) and neural rendering methods alongside classical 3D techniques
 Solve hard problems across calibration, multi-view geometry, optimization, and rendering at scale
 Move research-grade prototypes into reliable production systems
 Contribute across our stack in TypeScript, Python, and AWS
 Required qualifications
 PhD + 2 years of relevant experience, OR Master's + 5 years of relevant experience, OR strong startup experience shipping 3D technologies
 Direct, hands-on experience with 3D reconstruction (self-driving, robotics, AR/VR, or mapping)
 Solid grounding in 3D fundamentals: camera models, multi-view geometry, point clouds, meshes, SDFs
 Working familiarity with neural splatting (Gaussian Splatting, NeRF-family methods) and modern neural rendering
 Strong software engineering fundamentals — comfortable owning systems in production
 Proficient in Python and willing to work in TypeScript on AWS
 Preferred qualifications
 Experience at a self-driving car company, robotics company, or 3D-focused startup
 Track record of bringing strong colleagues with you — we deeply value candidates who can attract and refer other top talent
 Compensation & benefits
 Base salary: $300,000
 Equity in an early-stage company
