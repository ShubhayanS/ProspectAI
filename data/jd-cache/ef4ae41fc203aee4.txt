 2. About the Opportunity:
 The Data Engineer II is responsible for developing and operationalizing data pipelines that enable data access for reporting and advanced analytics. This role ensures data quality and reliability across environments and contributes to improving data processing efficiency. The position is integral to supporting the organization’s data infrastructure and analytics capabilities.
 3. Responsibilities:
 • Design and implement standardized data management procedures including data staging, ingestion, preparation, provisioning, and destruction
 • Develop, test, document, and operate large-scale, high-volume data structures for business intelligence
 • Build and maintain real-time processing applications and data pipelines
 • Ensure quality of technical solutions as data moves through environments
 • Provide insights and suggestions for adapting to changes in data environments and utilization needs
 • Develop and maintain data architectures using programming languages and tools
 • Identify opportunities to improve data reliability, efficiency, and quality, including automation
 • Perform other assigned duties
 • Comply with all policies and standards
 4. Requirements:
 • Bachelor’s degree in a quantitative or business field (e.g., statistics, mathematics, engineering, computer science) or equivalent experience
 • 2 to 4 years of relevant experience
 • Experience with one or more of the following: ETL, SQL, Snowflake
