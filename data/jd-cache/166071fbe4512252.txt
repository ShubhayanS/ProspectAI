 1. About Our Client:
 The organization operates in the digital sports platform industry, addressing the challenge of connecting sports fans worldwide with licensed merchandise, collectibles, and betting opportunities. It serves a vast global sports fan base through commerce, collectibles, and sports betting products. The company has a network of approximately 900 sports properties, including professional leagues, teams, athletes, and retail partners, along with more than 2,000 retail locations. Its sports betting subsidiary provides online and retail betting services across several U.S. states and runs multiple retail sportsbooks, including the only sportsbook inside an NFL stadium. The company employs over 22,000 people focused on enhancing the fan experience globally.
 2. About the Opportunity:
 The Analytics Engineer III will play a key role in the Data organization’s Analytics Engineering team by developing accurate reporting and generating insights that support business growth. Reporting to the Manager of Analytics Engineering, this position collaborates with internal and external stakeholders to build metrics and provide ongoing analysis and recommendations. The role is critical for creating data models and ensuring data accuracy, contributing directly to the organization''s decision-making and success.
 3. Responsibilities:
 • Develop a strong understanding of the sports betting and gaming products and operations
 • Design and build metric definitions, KPIs, and source of truth tables using advanced SQL
 • Own DBT cloud infrastructure and implement data modeling best practices
 • Collaborate with analysts and data engineering teams to solve business problems
 • Develop Snowflake Intelligence AI to support self-serve analytics
 • Stay current with data analytics technologies and industry trends
 4. Requirements:
 • Advanced SQL skills, including query and model optimization
 • Experience with data modeling using tools like DBT, including incremental modeling
 • Proficient in managing source code control with GitHub and best collaboration practices
 • Experience building dashboards and reports with tools such as Tableau, Sigma, Power BI, or Looker
 • Outcome-focused, data-driven, and experienced in high-growth environments
 • Proven ability to build trust and partner closely with stakeholders
 • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills; familiarity with AI in analytics is a plus
 • Excellent organizational and communication skills
 • Positive and adaptable attitude toward new initiatives
 • Knowledge of sports betting or customer behavior analysis is advantageous
 • Willingness to travel for team offsites and events
 • Minimum of 5 years of relevant experience
 5. Pay Range and Compensation Package:
 • Salary range: £72,000 – £72,000 British Pounds per year
 • Base salary varies based on job-related knowledge, skills, experience, and geographic location
 • Remote employees may be eligible for a home office setup stipend
