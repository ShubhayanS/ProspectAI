 Power BI & Data Analytics Lead
 We’re looking for a Power BI expert to lead the development of enterprise dashboards and drive data-driven decision-making across the business.
 This role will own Power BI reporting, data modeling, and KPI development , while partnering with teams across finance, operations, marketing, and e-commerce to turn data into actionable insights.
 What you’ll do:
 Build and optimize Power BI dashboards, reports, and semantic models
 Define and standardize key business metrics (margin, CAC, customer, operational KPIs)
 Enable self-service reporting and train end users
 Partner with stakeholders to translate business needs into scalable BI solutions
 Support underlying data pipelines and data models (Azure SQL, Snowflake, ERP systems)
 What you bring:
 5–8+ years in BI / analytics , with deep Power BI expertise (DAX, modeling, performance)
 Strong SQL skills (Azure SQL preferred)
 Experience with modern data warehouses ( Snowflake, Synapse, BigQuery )
 Background working with complex data environments and multiple data sources
