 Job Summary:
 We are seeking a highly skilled Data Engineer with strong expertise in Python, Data Modeling, and SQL . The ideal candidate will have experience working with document databases , building scalable data solutions, and designing systems with a focus on data privacy and visibility .
 Key Responsibilities:
 Design and develop scalable data pipelines and data models
 Work extensively with Python for data processing and engineering tasks
 Design and manage document databases and structured data systems
 Implement data privacy (visibility) controls in system design
 Develop and optimize SQL queries for performance and analytics
 Collaborate with cross-functional teams to understand data requirements
 Ensure data accuracy, consistency, and integrity across systems
 Required Skills & Experience:
 8+ years of experience in Data Engineering or related field
 Strong expertise in:
 Python
 Data Modeling
 SQL (advanced queries, optimization)
 Experience with Document Databases (e.g., MongoDB or similar)
 Strong understanding of data privacy and visibility design principles
 Excellent analytical and quantitative skills
 Skill Category:
 Data Engineering – Python, Data Modeling
 Eligibility:
 Visa: GC, US Citizen, L2, H4
 Additional Notes:
 Immediate joiners highly preferred
 Strong communication and problem-solving skills required
