 About Us
 ExperINS is a modern benefits brokerage built for growing businesses that want to take care of their people and fuel their growth. We help companies build bold benefit strategies that align with their business goals — and then deliver them with white-glove, people-first service.
 We’re not tied to insurance carriers. We sit on the same side of the table as our clients. We bring clarity, confidence, and strategy to an industry that’s often anything but.
 Job Description
 Enjoy turning messy data into clean, client-ready insights? Want to build systems that make work faster and smarter? We’re hiring a Data & RFP Analyst to support our Client Service team at ExperINS. This role sits at the center of our busiest work, managing RFPs, building high-quality Excel deliverables, and improving how we handle data across the business.
 The Data Analyst supports business growth and client retention by transforming data into actionable insights, improving internal processes, and delivering analytics that strengthen sales opportunities, renewal strategy, and ongoing client consulting.
 You’ll own critical workflows during peak seasons, while also helping us automate, streamline, and scale how we operate.
 This is not just execution. This is building better systems.
 What You’ll Do
 Core Execution (Day-to-Day)
 Partner with Sales team through diligence review. Prepare prospect and finalist claims and benchmarking reporting. Create executive-ready charts, exhibits and presentation materials for prospect and finalist presentations.
 Manage multiple RFPs at once and proposal processes through data gathering, formatting, quality review, and deadline coordination
 Develop renewal and prospect analytics including contribution modeling, plan comparisons, rate analysis, and cost projections
 Support service team by gathering data including plan performance materials, benchmarking, contribution strategies, and renewal projections
 Prepare client-ready reporting and presentation materials for pre-renewal, renewal, and strategic planning meetings
 Automation & Process Improvement (Core Focus)
 Build and maintain Excel macros, templates, and automated workflows
 Identify inefficiencies and redesign processes to reduce manual work
 Standardize templates, data inputs, and file structures across the team
 Improve turnaround time, consistency, and scalability of RFP execution
 Help create repeatable systems that make high-volume work easier to manage
 Data & Analytics (Growth Area)
 Support the building and ongoing management of internal SQL databases and data structures
 Write and use basic SQL queries to pull, validate, and organize data
 Use Python to automate repetitive tasks, improve data workflows, and support internal tools
 Partner with technical resources to expand automation and strengthen data infrastructure
 Help improve how ExperINS captures, stores, and uses data across the business
 Requirements
 Must-Have
 Advanced Excel skills, including XLOOKUP, INDEX/MATCH, pivot tables, and working with large datasets
 Experience building or using Excel macros and VBA
 Strong attention to detail and data accuracy
 Ability to manage multiple projects and deadlines at once
 Comfort working with both structured and messy data
 Interest in automation and process improvement
 Working knowledge of basic SQL queries
 Experience with Python for data cleanup, workflow support, or automation
 Nice-to-Have
 Experience with employee benefits and/or insurance
 Experience building or managing SQL databases
 Experience with process automation or workflow design
 Experience with reporting or visualization tools such as Power BI, Tableau, or Zoho Analytics
 Familiarity with RFPs or other client-facing deliverables
 Experience working with healthcare or employee benefits data
