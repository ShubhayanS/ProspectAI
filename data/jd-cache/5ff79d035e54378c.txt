 Job Title: Data Engineer – Healthcare
 Company: Aaratech Inc.
 Location: Dallas-Fort Worth Metroplex
 Work Authorization
 At this time, we are only able to consider candidates who are authorized to work in the United States without sponsorship.
 Job Summary
 Aaratech Inc. is seeking a Data Engineer with experience in the healthcare domain. The role involves designing, building, and maintaining data pipelines and systems to support healthcare data processing, analytics, and reporting.
 Key Responsibilities
 Design, develop, and maintain scalable data pipelines
 Integrate data from healthcare systems such as EHR/EMR
 Ensure data quality, consistency, and integrity
 Work with large datasets for analytics and reporting
 Collaborate with data analysts, business teams, and IT stakeholders
 Support data warehousing and ETL processes
 Ensure compliance with healthcare data regulations (HIPAA)
 Qualifications
 Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, IT, or related field
 Minimum 2 years of Data Engineering experience
 Experience in healthcare domain preferred
 Strong programming skills (Python, SQL)
 Experience with data pipelines and ETL processes
 Preferred Skills
 Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, or GCP)
 Knowledge of healthcare standards (HL7, FHIR)
 Experience with data warehouses (Snowflake, Redshift, BigQuery)
 Familiarity with Apache Spark or similar frameworks
 About Aaratech Inc.
 Aaratech Inc. is a technology-driven organization focused on delivering innovative solutions across healthcare and enterprise domains. We are committed to improving operational efficiency and enabling better outcomes through data-driven insights and collaborative partnerships.
