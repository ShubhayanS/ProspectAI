 1. About Our Client:
 This organization operates one of the internet’s largest platforms for open and authentic conversations, hosting over 100,000 active communities and approximately 121 million daily active unique visitors. It addresses the challenge of helping diverse user groups discover, connect, and engage through personalized content and community experiences. Machine learning is central to how the organization powers personalized recommendations, search, advertising optimization, and marketplace dynamics at scale.
 2. About the Opportunity:
 The Machine Learning Engineer will design and build production-grade machine learning systems that enhance core user experiences across the platform. This role is responsible for developing models and infrastructure that improve content discovery, relevance, advertising effectiveness, and user engagement. The position directly impacts user experience, advertiser value, and business outcomes by applying advanced ML and AI techniques at internet scale.
 3. Responsibilities:
 • Design, build, and deploy scalable machine learning models and systems
 • Manage the full ML lifecycle from problem definition to monitoring
 • Develop reliable and observable data and model pipelines with automated retraining
 • Work with large-scale datasets to enhance ranking, recommendations, search relevance, and optimization systems
 • Collaborate cross-functionally with Product, Data Science, Infrastructure, and Engineering teams
 • Optimize system performance for latency, throughput, and model quality
 • Research and implement state-of-the-art ML and AI methods, including deep learning and LLM evaluation/alignment
 • Contribute to technical strategy, architecture, and long-term ML roadmap
 4. Requirements:
 • 3-5+ years of experience building and operating production machine learning systems
 • Strong programming skills in Python, Java, Go, or similar languages
 • Solid understanding of ML algorithms, including statistical learning and deep learning architectures
 • Hands-on experience with ML frameworks such as PyTorch or TensorFlow
 • Experience designing scalable ML pipelines, data processing, and model serving infrastructure
 • Ability to translate complex product or business problems into ML solutions
 • Proven track record of improving metrics through applied machine learning
 Preferred Qualifications:
 • Experience with recommender, search/ranking, advertising, or large-scale representation learning systems
 • Familiarity with distributed systems and large-scale data processing tools (e.g., Spark, Kafka, BigQuery)
 • Experience in real-time, low-latency production environments
 • Background in feature engineering, model optimization, and production monitoring
 • Knowledge of LLM/Gen AI techniques, including evaluation, alignment, fine-tuning, and productionizing LLM-powered products
 • Advanced degree in Computer Science, Machine Learning, or related quantitative field
 5. Pay Range and Compensation Package:
 • The pay range and compensation package for this role will be determined based on the candidate’s experience, skills, and other relevant factors.
 6. Benefits & Perks:
 • Comprehensive healthcare benefits and income replacement programs
 • 401(k) with employer match
 • Global benefit programs including workspace, professional development, and caregiving support
 • Family planning support
 • Gender-affirming care
 • Mental health and coaching benefits
 • Flexible vacation and paid volunteer time off
 • Generous paid parental leave
