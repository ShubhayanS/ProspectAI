About Us:
 Temu is seeking a highly motivated and experienced Data Engineer to join our team. As a rapidly growing company, we are looking for talented individuals committed to helping us achieve our goals. In this role, you will have the opportunity to work with a team of driven individuals dedicated to making a difference in the industry.
 Responsibilities:
 Establish a robust business metric framework tied to the overarching goals of the business team;
 Analyze business processes to identify challenges and opportunities, providing data-driven recommendations for strategic business decisions, product development, and operational optimization;
 Formalize analytical frameworks, translate business needs into concrete data requirements, and collaborate with cross-functional teams (e.g., Product, Engineering) to drive business impact and commercial value.
 Job Requirements:
 Minimum of 3 years of data engineering experience;
 Experience with data modeling, warehousing and building ETL pipelines;
 Experience with SQL;
 Self-motivated, detail-oriented, strong communication and teamwork skills
 BS/MS in Computer Science, Math, Economics, Statistics, or other science and engineering fields.
