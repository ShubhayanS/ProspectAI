 Scientific Data Analyst (Junior) – Public Health Analytics
 📍 Atlanta, GA (Hybrid – Onsite 2–3 days/week)
 💼 W2 Contract
 We are seeking a Junior Scientific Data Analyst to support epidemiology and healthcare quality analytics initiatives under senior scientific guidance.
 This role focuses on structured scientific data work , reproducible analytics, and documentation used in public‑health surveillance and reporting workflows.
 Role Overview
 The Scientific Data Analyst will support investigators and senior analysts working with healthcare surveillance datasets. Responsibilities are execution‑focused and centered on data quality, repeatable analysis workflows, and documented analytical outputs.
 The position requires onsite presence in Atlanta for secure dataset access and collaboration.
 Key Responsibilities
 Perform data cleaning, validation, and quality control on healthcare surveillance datasets
 Develop and maintain well‑documented R and/or Python scripts for data preparation and analysis
 Produce descriptive analyses, summary tables, and trend outputs under senior guidance
 Maintain data dictionaries, change logs, and study documentation
 Support preparation of tables and figures for internal scientific reviews and reporting
 Assist with data preparation for modeling and structured analytics tasks as requested
 Participate in program meetings and onsite working sessions
 Required Qualifications
 Master’s degree (Public Health, Epidemiology, Biostatistics, Health Informatics, Data Science, or related field)
 1–3 years of relevant experience (academic research, internships, or professional experience acceptable)
 Working knowledge of R and/or Python with emphasis on clean, reproducible code
 Experience working with structured datasets and repeatable QC workflows
 Strong documentation and organizational skills
 Proficiency with Microsoft Office tools (Excel, Word, PowerPoint).
 Preferred Experience
 Exposure to public‑health surveillance or healthcare analytics
 Basic SQL skills for data extraction and reconciliation
 Experience supporting scientific or structured reporting workflows
 Additional Details
 Work Authorization: USC, GC, CPT‑40, OPT, OPT‑EXT, H4‑EAD
 Employment Type: W2 only (no C2C / no 1099)
 Compensation: Competitive, based on experience and interview feedback
 Duration: Long‑term contract through December 2027, with potential extension based on program needs
