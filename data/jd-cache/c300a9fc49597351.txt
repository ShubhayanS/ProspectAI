 About The Company
 Optum, a subsidiary of UnitedHealth Group, is a global organization dedicated to transforming healthcare through innovative solutions and technology. Committed to improving health outcomes worldwide, Optum leverages data, analytics, technology, and expertise to deliver comprehensive care, pharmacy benefits, and health services. The company fosters a culture rooted in diversity and inclusion, providing talented professionals with opportunities for growth and development. With a focus on health equity, Optum aims to make healthcare more accessible, equitable, and effective for all communities. The organization emphasizes responsible innovation, ensuring that advancements in AI and machine learning are aligned with ethical standards and regulatory compliance.
 About The Role
 We are seeking a highly skilled AI/ML Scientist to join Optum AI, the enterprise AI team within UnitedHealth Group. This role offers an exciting opportunity to develop groundbreaking AI solutions tailored for healthcare applications, utilizing both structured and unstructured data. The successful candidate will be instrumental in designing, building, and deploying advanced machine learning models that impact millions of lives. You will collaborate with top-tier researchers, engineers, and business stakeholders to create innovative solutions that enhance healthcare delivery, optimize operations, and support health equity initiatives. The role provides flexibility to work remotely within the United States, with specific in-office requirements for candidates based in Minneapolis or Washington, D.C.
 Qualifications
 MS in Computer Science, Data Science, or related quantitative field with 5+ years of industry or academic experience in AI/ML, or PhD with 2+ years of relevant experience
 Proven expertise in AI/ML techniques, including deep learning and classical machine learning methods
 Extensive experience with Python programming language
 Hands-on experience with deep learning frameworks such as PyTorch or TensorFlow
 Strong understanding of distributed computing environments and tools
 Excellent communication skills for technical and non-technical audiences
 Ability to collaborate effectively within multidisciplinary teams
 Responsibilities
 Develop innovative AI approaches for healthcare applications, utilizing both structured and unstructured data sources
 Design, build, and evaluate deep learning and traditional machine learning models in distributed computing environments
 Plan and conduct experiments, perform statistical analyses, and create compelling data visualizations to communicate findings
 Engage with business stakeholders to understand needs and develop AI-driven solutions that address real-world challenges
 Collaborate with the Responsible Use of AI (RUAI) team to ensure compliance with ethical standards and company policies
 Document code, models, patents, and publish research findings in scientific journals and conferences
 Work alongside engineering teams to establish robust MLOps pipelines and data workflows for scalable deployment
 Keep abreast of the latest advancements in AI/ML research, tools, and methodologies
 Benefits
 Competitive salary range of $106,800 to $194,200 annually, commensurate with experience and qualifications
 Comprehensive benefits package including health, dental, and vision insurance
 Incentive and recognition programs
 Equity stock purchase plans
 401(k) retirement savings plan with company contributions
 Flexible remote work options within the U.S., with in-office requirements for certain locations
 Opportunities for professional development, training, and career growth
 Equal Opportunity

We provide consideration for employment without regard to race, ethnicity, gender, sexual orientation, age, disability, religion, veteran status, or any other protected characteristic under applicable laws.
