 Direct message the job poster from The Emerald Recruiting Group
 Christopher Sullivan
 Christopher Sullivan
 President at The Emerald Recruiting Group
 A sophisticated quantitative investment firm is seeking a Machine Learning Researcher to develop ML/DL models designed to generate alpha across large-scale financial and alternative datasets. You’ll work directly with Portfolio Managers and senior quantitative researchers in a fast-moving research environment where ideas are tested quickly, judged ruthlessly, and funded when they work. A rare concept in modern life.
 This role is ideal for someone who can bridge advanced machine learning techniques with real market application, rather than building elegant models that collapse on contact with reality.
 Responsibilities
 Develop and enhance machine learning / deep learning models for alpha generation across equities, futures, macro, or multi-asset strategies
 Research predictive signals using structured and unstructured financial and alternative datasets
 Build feature engineering pipelines across high-volume datasets
 Design and run robust backtests, validation frameworks, and out-of-sample testing methodologies
 Work closely with PMs and quant researchers to refine signals and integrate models into live trading strategies
 Improve model performance, interpretability, and production stability
 Monitor live models and adapt to changing market regimes
 Stay current on new ML techniques and identify practical investment applications
 Requirements
 Advanced degree in Computer Science, Statistics, Mathematics, Physics, Engineering, or related quantitative field
 Strong experience building ML/DL models in production or research environments
 Proficiency in Python and common ML frameworks such as PyTorch, TensorFlow, XGBoost, or similar
 Strong grasp of statistics, optimization, and experimental design
 Experience with large datasets and distributed compute environments
 Exposure to financial markets, systematic investing, or alpha research strongly preferred
 Ability to communicate research findings clearly to technical and investment stakeholders
 Preferred Background
 Experience with NLP, time-series forecasting, reinforcement learning, or graph-based models
 Familiarity with market microstructure, execution data, or portfolio construction concepts
 Track record of signals or models that improved live performance
 Why This Role
 Direct impact on investment performance
 Tight research-feedback loop where strong ideas move quickly
 Access to large proprietary datasets and serious compute resources
 Work alongside elite PMs and researchers without ten committees asking for slides no one reads
 Ideal Candidate
 Someone intellectually aggressive, commercially aware, and comfortable being wrong often enough to eventually be very right. Markets tend to humble everyone eventually. The trick is billing them first.
