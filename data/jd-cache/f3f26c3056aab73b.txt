 🚀 We're Hiring: AI Engineer (Agentic AI / LLM Systems) 🚀
 📍 Location: Global Remote
 🕒 Working Hours: Must overlap with US East Coast (EST)
 Our client, a fast-growing AI-driven technology company, is looking for an AI Engineer to help build and deploy the next generation of agentic AI systems and intelligent automation .
 If you’re excited about LLMs, autonomous agents, and real-world AI applications , this could be the perfect opportunity to work on cutting-edge products with a global team.
 What You’ll Do
 • Design, develop, and deploy AI-driven systems and autonomous agents .
 • Build and optimize LLM-powered applications and AI workflows.
 • Develop scalable AI infrastructure and integrate models into production systems.
 • Work closely with product and engineering teams to implement AI solutions across multiple products.
 • Stay up to date with the latest advancements in agentic AI, generative AI, and machine learning frameworks .
 Requirements
 ✅ Strong experience building AI/ML systems in production environments .
 ✅ Experience with LLMs, generative AI, or agent frameworks (LangChain, LlamaIndex, AutoGen, etc.).
 ✅ Proficiency in Python and modern AI tooling.
 ✅ Experience with vector databases, RAG pipelines, or AI orchestration frameworks is highly desirable.
 ✅ Comfortable working remotely with distributed global teams .
 ✅ Must be able to work US East Coast hours .
 Why You’ll Love This Role
 🌍 Fully remote — work from anywhere globally
 🤖 Work on cutting-edge AI and agentic systems
 🚀 Join a high-growth, innovative tech environment
 💰 Competitive salary: $80,000 – $130,000 USD depending on location and experience
 ✨ Opportunity to help shape the future of AI-powered products
 We look forward to your application.
